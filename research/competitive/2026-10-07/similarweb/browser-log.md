## Verification and exclusions

- Similarweb loads asynchronously: immediate snapshots after changing a URL sometimes still contained the previous domain or incomplete cards. Those reads were discarded. Later reads waited for the actual target profile link/domain and the loaded result. An early helper's verified flag alone was not considered enough.
- Subsidiary panels that did not finish loading were left blank/null rather than inferred. aion2.tools and Inven have incomplete channel records.
- Aion2Atlas text record showed 7,737 Visits with the correct profile. A later partial snapshot lacked the card; the previously loaded card was retained.
- Fextralife focus interruption was recovered by reading the existing tab, then checking its filtered rows.
- Large landing reports: first 100 rows reviewed for AION2 Hub (112 URLs) and filtered Fextralife (150 URLs); Questlog and Gamers4 also reviewed on their first 100 rows. No claim of all-page coverage.
- Query variants such as ysclid were kept as raw URLs in the transcription, not treated as distinct topics or blindly summed.
- Counts with K/M are rounded display values. visitsApprox is a unit conversion of the UI label, not additional precision. Clicks under 50 remain '< 50'; shares under 0.01 remain '< 0.01%'.
- aion2guide.org: domain Visits 607, whereas the organic table has 350/21.60% on home and other rows adding substantially more than 607. This inconsistency remains unresolved. Modules must not be calibrated against one another.
- New/small domains: daily averages, device distribution, engagement and percentage readings may rely on limited observed days. No full-month extrapolation made.
- Questlog Portuguese-URL class guide showed 13.7K clicks from one English keyword. Treated as an anomalous report row; not evidence that Portuguese translation should take priority.
- The raw keywords were preserved as observed, including spelling errors and non-English queries. No new keywords added to the project lexicon.
- Sources include the exact Similarweb URLs per record in data.json and CSVs. Statistics refer to web visits/search estimates, not Global/KR/TW player counts.
- Local scheduled rift record is unconfirmed. Recommendations to add a timer are conditional on future official schedule verification, not confirmation of current game mechanics.
- No outreach, publishing, signup, payment, uploads or account setting changes were performed.
