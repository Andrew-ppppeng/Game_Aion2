Can You Play AION 2 on Mac or Steam Deck?
The global version is Windows PC only, through Steam and PURPLE. Mac, Steam Deck and other non-Windows devices have no official support.
AION 2 Beta: The Launch Scale Test Is Over
Global had one test: the Launch Scale Test on September 17-18, six hours a day, no keys, level cap 37, progress wiped. Founder's Pack is now the only early route.
AION 2 on PS5: Confirmed in Development, Not at Launch
AION 2 is not on PS5 at launch; it arrives on PC on 5 October 2026. NC's development PD confirmed in July 2026 that a PS5 version is in development, with no date.
AION 2 Founder's Pack Guide: 24.99 vs 49.99 vs 99.99

AION 2 FAQ
Release, platforms, classes, dungeons, monetization and PC specs - every answer traces back to an official page.
When does AION 2 launch?
October 5, 2026 worldwide, on PC only - Steam and PURPLE. A Founder's Pack gets you in five days earlier, from September 30.
See the full timeline
Can I play it on PS5 or mobile?
Launch is PC only. NC's development PD confirmed a PS5 version is in development but gave no date. Mobile exists only in Korea and Taiwan; the global version officially has none.
See platform status
How many classes are there?
The official Korean class guide says eight. Global launch is expected to be those eight. The ninth, Brawler, shipped only in Korea and Taiwan.
See the class list
Will Brawler be in the global launch?
NC has published no global roster as of September 18, 2026. Creators who play the Korea/Taiwan service believe global launches with eight and no Brawler - but that is a creator report, not an official announcement.
See how Brawler plays
Can my PC run it?
Minimum is Ryzen 5 2600 / i5-10500, 8 GB RAM and GTX 1050 Ti (4GB). Recommended is Ryzen 7 3700X / i7-11700, 16 GB and RTX 2070 (8GB). Both tiers need 100 GB and both recommend an SSD.
See the spec table
Is AION 2 pay to win?
It is free to play with an optional USD 15/month membership. The membership unlocks the Market and the Kina-to-Quna exchange. The shop is cosmetics and consumables, which NC says do not affect power. Whether buying Kina with real money crosses your line is your call.
See the full breakdown
Are the Founder's Packs worth it?
All three include five days of early access, a supply chest, a unique title and 30 days of membership. The extra tiers are entirely cosmetics - no power. Worth it only if you want those looks or the day-one head start.
Compare the three tiers
Is there another beta I can join?
No. The only test was the Launch Scale Test on September 17-18, six hours a day, no keys, progress wiped. The only way to play earlier now is Founder's Pack early access.
