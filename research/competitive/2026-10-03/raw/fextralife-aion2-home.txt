Aion 2 Guide
What is Aion 2?
Aion 2
is a Fantasy online MMO action RPG developed by NCsoft. The game is an extension, continuation, and a revamp of the setting and core game mechanics initially introduced in the original title Aion in 2008. Aion 2 is utilizing Unreal Engine 5 allowing for the development team to craft a world and environments with rich detail and dynamic lighting. Players will engage in fluid, real-time combat with an action basis. Dodging, strategic strikes, and chained combos are important to winning the day. While the original title came out over a decade ago, Aion 2 aims to revamp the action-adventure experience of the franchise while connecting players in both PvE and PvP play.
As with most MMORPGs, Aion 2 allows for deep player customization beginning with character creation and class selection. The game features eight classes to choose from. Those classes are as follows:
Gladiator
:
(DPS) Strong AoE attacks and melee combat-focused
Templar
:
(Tank) Armed with a shield and capable of strong defensive skills and strategies.
Cleric
:
(Support) Primarily a healer with combat as a secondary focus.
Chanter
:
(Support) Offers multifaceted buff supports and potential healing of allies.
Ranger
:
(Ranged DPS) Utilizes long-range weaponry and traps.
Assassin
:
(Stealth) Agile and stealthy capable of burst damage from behind.
Sorcerer
:
(Mage) Magic-focused class specializing in dealing significant magic-based damage.
Elementalist
:
(Mage) Summoner of spirits capable of aiding in battle.

Classes in Aion 2
Gladiator
A melee powerhouse with impactful wide-area attacks. Attacks are slow, but damaging. This class excels at frontline combat, dealing high damage.
Templar
The templar wields a shield and is capable of absorbing significant damage with defensive maneuvers. They are ideal for holding the enemy's attention.
Assassin
The assassin is agile and deals high-burst damage. This class is ideal for those looking to play the field with hit-and-run strikes.
Ranger
Skilled in long-range combat, the ranger is best utilized by those aiming for precision and impact.
Sorcerer
This magic-wielder delivers devastating damage utilizing elemental magic.
Elementalist
This class summons elemental spirits to accompany them into battle.
Cleric
Clerics act as healers and will provide supporting buffs to allies.
Chanter
This class offers buffs for allies both offensively and defensively in the midst of battle.

Korean Registration Announcement
On October 2nd, NCsoft Korea and Taiwan revealed their plans for Server and Character Name reservations for players registering in Korea. The post included a short guide on when the reservation period will be available, how Korean players can access it, and what aspects of character creation can be pre-selected. We will summarize the information shared below:
Registration Period
Players will be able to register for Aion 2 from
Thursday, October 16th 2025, at 14:00 KST
until Monday, January 20th 2026.
Registration will be available until servers are full.
To preregister, players simply need to access the official Aion 2 Website . No game download will be necessary.
How to Register
Log in with your PLAYNC Account
Choose your Faction
Select one of the available servers
Reserve your Character Name
Important Notes for Registration
Servers are divided by faction.
In normal moment-to-moment gameplay, you will not encounter players in other factions outside of PvP battles in the Abyss, which employ 1:1 Server Matching.
Once your server and Character Name have been selected in registration, you cannot change either of them at a later date. As such, please choose wisely.

September Livestream
Aion 2 Developers provided an in depth update on various factors of Aion 2 during their recent livestream. They covered a great deal of topics, which we’ll summarise below.
Gameplay
Dungeons & PVP
Dungeons can be instantly jumped into via teleportation. This is to ensure that people can level up without being attacked in front of dungeon entrances.This ensures leveling isn’t interrupted.
PVP encounters while leveling are being replaced with dedicated PVP content.
Combat on Mobile
There is no full auto combat. However, there are combat assistance features that aim to help mobile players during fights.
Combat assistance makes the game more accessible and increases enjoyability for mobile players. Players still have to interact and actually play the game.
Without this feature, mobile would likely be considered too hard to play.
Abyss Zone PVP
PvP zone is accessible for 7 hours per week, this timer can be extended with items.
Death & Revival
Depending on the character's death, certain conditions apply to revival. In the case of a PVE death, revival items can be used, whereas with a PVP death, a revival crystal cannot be used if the player is killed by an enemy faction. Kisks can be used to set a respawn point, functioning similarly to Aion 1.
Factions
Elyos and Asmodians are similar now, compared with Aion1. There are still some differences, but these are not exaggerated.
Mounts & Housing
Mounts can be pets that will carry the player character.
Housing allows the player to display their achievements and reward statues.
UI Feedback
Players have highlighted concerns that the UI might look mobile focussed. The developers took time to reassure players that the game is PC first, and that the UI is not fully polished due to rushing (to get ready for the open beta). UI changes have been made since the last broadcast to move towards a PC feeling UI.

Currency System
Aion 2 will have two currencies available, Kinah, the in-game currency, and Quna, the shop currency. The Auction house uses Kinah only. In addition, trading will be possible between Kinah and Quna, this will be a player driven exchange, and ratios are determined by the player economy.
Monetization
Aion 2 will be Free to play, and will have subscription and battle pass options. In addition:
The in game cash shop includes cosmetics, outfits, weapon skins, pets, wings.
Pets can be both earned during gameplay or bought in store.
A wardrobe system will be available for skin usage.
Players can use the Trading post to purchase consumables and limited bags. Enchantment stones will not be available.
Release and Pre-Launch

The official launch date has been confirmed as November 19th, 2025 (or 18th depending on your timezone).
Pre-Download & Pre-Character Creation opens November 16–18, 2025.
Pre-registration rewards include Kinah and a mount (no phone required).

FAQs
When Does Aion 2 Release?
The game released in Korea and Taiwan in November 19th, 2025. It is currently scheduled for a global release mid-2026.
What Kind of Setting Does Aion 2 Have?
The game is set in the world of Atreia many years following the original game. In this world, players will journey across lands filled with lush vegetation and wild fauna in mystical realms such as Elyos as well as more melancholic and oppressive settings like the Asmodian domain. Players will be able to traverse the land using flight, water traversal, and more from various mounts.
What Multiplayer Options are Available?
The game features both PvE and PvP play. Little is yet known about the extent of this options. However, the two primary realms will be warring with one another across PvP matches. Players can then tackle the more difficult challenges the world offers with allies in PvE zones throughout the map. Within each world, there are Expedition events and World events that players can join in teams to fulfill a particular challenge and earn rewards. These events are spread out across the open world.
Who is developing Aion 2?
Aion 2 is developed by NCSoft, a South Korean developer responsible for the original 2008 title Aion. The team also worked on such titles like Lineage and Blade & Soul.
What Platforms Will Aion 2 Be Available On?
Aion 2 is currently set to release on PC. Console platforms are unconfirmed.

Aion 2 Focus Group Testing
NCSoft held a Focus Group Test at their Headquarters in South Korea. It was an invite only event, under NDA, however, feedback has been released in various locations and by different means. The testers came from a variety of backgrounds, so feedback is varied and from multiple different perspectives. As part of the test, the players were able to experience the levelling process until around level 21, then were boosted to level 45. Despite some preconceptions, and potential reputational impact from previous developer games and actions, first impressions, world design, and combat system received notably positive reactions overall.
Please note, the below is summarised key points, gathered through verified testers, streamers, and Korean community chats.
General Impressions
Runs on Unreal Engine 5 and features rich, detailed environments.
Customization is flexible, extensive, and true to in-game models.
Combat feels like a hybrid of Aion, Blade & Soul, and Black Desert.
Skill count is limited, partly due to mobile UI design.
Leveling is fast and smooth, with players reaching level 45 easily during the test.
Adventure elements and dungeon rankings offer meaningful progression and rewards.
Exploration is a highlight; hidden dungeons, rich landscapes, and scaling zones keep old areas relevant.
Dungeon re-entry and rerun systems resemble pay-to-win mechanics familiar to Korean audiences.
Crafting materials may be tradable, raising pay-to-win risks.
The devs claim the current monetization is 2–3 months old but may remain unchanged.
Performance was smooth on high-end systems, with rare frame drops during map transitions.
aion 2 wiki world overview1
PvE Specific Feedback
Leveling was smooth and enjoyable, with a satisfying progression curve.
Dungeons were cinematic and well-designed, with engaging boss encounters.
Skill chaining felt fluid and fun, especially in party play.
Exploration was rich, with hidden content and a sense of adventure.
4-man party content encouraged cooperation and role synergy.
Combat felt “floaty” or lacked impact, especially with basic attacks.
Some testers found the auto-lock targeting too passive compared to full action systems.
A few felt the controls were awkward; though others suggested it might just come down to player skill.
Early-game tutorials were lacking, likely due to the age of the test build (2–3 months old).
PvP Specific Feedback
1v1 and 4v4 arenas exist and appeared to be functional.
Class synergy and defined roles were present and received some praise.
Wings disrupt combat; players can escape fights too easily.
Characters die too quickly, making battles feel shallow.
Controls felt overly simplified, reducing depth and skill expression.
No open-world dueling, which disappointed fans of classic Aion.
In general, PvP was seen as “fun in concept, underwhelming in execution.” With improvements to flight mechanics, combat responsiveness, and skill depth, it has potential, but it currently falls short of the expectations set by Aion 1.
Overall, reception among testers is cautiously optimistic: the game has high potential, but UI, combat balance, and monetization need work. NCSoft is targeting a November 11, 2025 release (matching Aion 1’s launch date), though this could shift slightly.
