AION 2 — September 30, 2026 Update Notes: Artifact Occupation War Schedule Changes, and a Time Limit for the Dimensional Core
The September 30 update, titled "Chapter 1. The Land of Sand and Frost", is a small Abyss-focused patch. Artifact Occupation War start times move to 9:20 / 9:50 / 10:20 PM for Groups 1 / 2 / 3 (still Wednesday and Saturday), and to curb marathon fights, a Dimensional Core that is not killed within 8 minutes of the battle starting receives a new "Waning Power" debuff that lowers its Damage Tolerance and Double Resist. The Abyss bosses that follow the war in Chaotic Lower and Middle Reshanta are pushed back to 9:45 / 10:15 / 10:45 PM, Guardian General Nachma's spawn times shift for Groups 2 and 3, and Enraged Guardian General Nachma now correctly receives Waning Power. The only class change is a pair of Sorcerer tooltip corrections, plus four fixes: Fire Temple's Black Smoke Murute jump landing, two Snowfield of Sorrow Triton exploits, and the Hidden Cube sound range.

AION 2 — Inside Client Build 120: Auto-Sync Arrives, a Class Conversion Service Waits, and the Outland Map Fills In
The September 30 client is a ~18 GB download with almost nothing new in it (0.5% of asset paths), and no patch note. What it does carry: Auto-Sync for Soul Bind attunement with ten strings and no announcement, a full Class Conversion window set with tables but no text, a new "Trust" HUD meter, and the Outland map growing from 11 files to 526. Everything already covered here is left out, and every item says whether it is new in this build or has been sitting in the client for weeks.

AION 2 Global Advanced Access Starts September 30 — Free Launch on October 5
AION 2 Global opens Advanced Access on September 30, 2026 (13:00 UTC) for Founder's Pack owners and launches free-to-play for everyone on October 5 at 13:00 UTC. Servers are announced for Europe, NA West, NA East, Latin America and Asia, each faction on its own server and paired with an opposing-faction server for the Abyss. Three Founder's Packs ($24.99 / $49.99 / $99.99) all include 5-day early access and a 30-day membership; higher tiers add cosmetics, a Black Dragon pet and wings. Pre-download is open on Steam and PURPLE; Steam players who joined the Launch Scale Test must uninstall the Playtest app.
