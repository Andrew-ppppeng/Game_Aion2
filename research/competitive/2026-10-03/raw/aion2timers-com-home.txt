Welcome to Aion 2 Timers
Aion 2 Spacetime Rift Timer
This is a live
Aion 2 Spacetime Rift timer
. The big countdown shows exactly when the next rift portal opens on your server, converted to your own time zone by your browser, so it stays correct through daylight-saving changes. While a rift is active it switches to the portal-close countdown, because the entry portal only stays open for about ten minutes.
Aion 2 Field Boss Timers
Every
Aion 2 field boss
(also called a world boss) on one page: respawn countdowns for your server, kept up to date by players and the AbyssLogs DPS Meter, and the scheduled Reshanta (Abyss) spawns counted down in your local time.
Field bosses have a preset spawn timer that starts the moment they are killed. Aion2Timers is partnered with
AbyssLogs DPS Meter
to send data to Aion2Timers to provide realtime feed to this Field Boss Tracker. It will monitor and track Field Boss kills and respawns while you're out and about. You can also setup a Discord alert for when servers respawn from the
Discord alerts page
.
Aion 2 Daily Reset and Weekly Reset Time
The
Aion 2 daily reset
happens at
05:00 server time
and the
weekly reset
on
Wednesday at 05:00 server time
. The clocks above show the countdown to both in your local time, and the Checklist (in the menu) clears itself automatically when each reset passes.
What resets daily
Mission quests (5 per day), Nightmare solo boss (2 per day), Expedition: Conquest (3 per day).
Transcendence charges (+1 per day, cap 14), Shugo Festival keys, Dimensional Invasion charges (+1 per day, cap 7).
Daily supply requests and the Black Cloud Merchant hourly stock cycle.
What resets weekly (Wednesday 05:00)
Command Missions (12), 4-player raids (3), the Ladra 10-player raid (2), Ascension Test (3), Expedition: Exploration (7), Odyle Energy Morph (7).
Daily dungeon entries: 7 free per week (up to 30 with tickets). They do not require daily logins, so you can clear all seven in one sitting before Wednesday.
Abyss weekly reward caps, Vakron Trial Subjugation Marks, Sanctuary and Snowfield of Sorrow boss clears, Shugo Festival keys (7 per week, 14 with membership).
Server time is UTC+9 on Korea and UTC+8 on Taiwan. Global NA, SA, EU and Japan servers open October 5, 2026 and their reset clock has not been published yet; this page will be updated the moment it is.

Aion 2 Discord Alerts
Post
Aion 2 alerts to your Discord server
with nothing more than a webhook URL. Pick the events below, set how many minutes before each one you want the ping, paste the webhook, and our alert server does the rest. The preview shows the exact message first. Unlike a browser alert it keeps working when this page is closed, and you can add a live timer board: one message in your channel that keeps itself up to date. Prefer asking a bot? The Aion 2 Timers bot answers
!timer rift
in any channel.
What you can be alerted about
Spacetime Rift openings (with the portal-close time in the message), Rift Domination, Abyss Rift Zone, Artifact Conquest and Abyss battlefield hours.
Reshanta world bosses: the post-Conquest bosses, Enraged Guardian Lord Nahma and the Watcher Kaira cycle.
Shugo Festival, Dimensional Invasion, Beritra Air Raid and the Black Cloud Merchant refresh (hourly, so enable with care).
Daily and weekly resets, and the global launch milestones.
How it works
Messages use Discord's own timestamp format, so every member sees the time in their local zone. You can ping @here, @everyone or a specific role, and field boss respawn alerts can be narrowed to the bosses you pick. The webhook is stored outside our web root, used only for these posts, and disabled automatically if Discord rejects it three times. Remove it here any time or delete the webhook in Discord. To create one: channel settings, Integrations, Webhooks, New Webhook, Copy Webhook URL.
Aion 2 OBS Stream Overlay
Put a live
Aion 2 timer on your stream
. The builder below writes a browser-source URL for OBS Studio, Streamlabs Desktop, StreamElements or any app that can load a web page, and shows you a scaled preview as you change options. Everything is carried in the URL, so the overlay needs no login and works on a second streaming PC.
Overlay layouts
Rift countdown
: the big Spacetime Rift timer with portal open, active and closed states.
Ticker bar
: one line for the top or bottom edge at 1920 by 72, showing what is live now and the next few events.
Next-up cards
: a grid of upcoming events you choose.
Checklist
: your daily and weekly progress, synced with the main page.
Twitch Chat: What To Play? (Wheel)
: viewers type !spin in your Twitch chat and the class wheel spins on stream, with rigged spins, Wolves' Method, faction, region, P2W and yes/no wheels.
Twitch Chat: What To Play? (Slot)
: the same chat commands on the What To Play? slot machine, typed as !pull, with its picks added to the same running totals as the wheel.
Twitch Chat: Aion 2 Trivia
: your chat answers Aion 2 questions by typing A, B, C or D, faster right answers score more, and every channel keeps an all-time leaderboard.
Options that matter on stream
Transparent, faint-card or text-only backgrounds, a size slider for crisp text at 1440p, text shadow for readability over gameplay, an accent colour to match your brand, times in the streaming PC's own time zone, 12 or 24-hour clock from your Settings, and an optional in-OBS sound alert with its own lead time. The overlay detects when it runs inside OBS, hides the cursor and scrollbars, and re-syncs the moment its scene becomes visible. Untick "Shutdown source when not visible" so the countdown keeps running between scenes.
Aion 2 Classes and Builds
Aion 2 has
9 playable classes
: Gladiator, Templar, Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Cleric, Chanter and Brawler. Every one of them is available to both factions and can be played as male or female, so picking a class never decides which side you land on. The panel above pulls each class straight from NCSOFT's official game data, with its skills, stigmas and specialisations under official English names.
Picking a class
We do not publish a tier list. Aion 2 balance moves with every patch, and a list written today is wrong by the next maintenance. What this page gives you instead is the actual data: the full skill list for each class, what each skill does at which level, the stigma options, and the specialisation trees, so you can judge a class on how it plays rather than on where somebody ranked it last month.
Builds and planning
The
Aion 2 Build Planner
lets you set a level, pick skills and stigmas, fit gear at any enhancement level and see the stat totals, then share the whole build as a link. The
stat calculators
cover the maths behind combat power, and
the database
holds every weapon, armour piece and accessory with stats at any enhancement level.
Where the class data comes from
Class, skill and specialisation data is mirrored from NCSOFT's official endpoints, not datamined or hand-typed, and refreshed daily. Skill descriptions come from NCSOFT's official Korean guidebook, machine translated. The official pages carry no damage numbers, ranges or cooldowns, so we do not show any — if you see those figures quoted elsewhere as official, they are not.
Aion 2 Schedule Today
This is the full
Aion 2 schedule for today
, converted from server time into your own time zone by your browser, so it stays correct through daylight-saving changes on both the Korea (UTC+9) and Taiwan (UTC+8) services.
What repeats, and when
Spacetime Rift
: every 3 hours, 8 times a day, at 02:00, 05:00, 08:00, 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 20:00 and 23:00 server time. The entry portal stays open about ten minutes.
Daily reset
: 05:00 server time.
Weekly reset
: Wednesday at 05:00 server time.
World bosses, Shugo Festival, Dimensional Invasion and Beritra Air Raid
: each on its own rotation, all listed above in your local time.
Why a schedule beats a single countdown
One countdown tells you what is next. A day view tells you whether it is worth staying up — whether the rift at 23:00 lands before or after your reset, whether a boss and a rift collide, and what you would miss by logging off now. Pick your server in Settings (the gear button) and the whole day re-converts.

FAQ
Frequently asked questions
When does the next Spacetime Rift open in Aion 2?
+
Spacetime Rifts open every 3 hours, 8 times a day, at 02:00, 05:00, 08:00, 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 20:00 and 23:00 server time. The entry portal stays open about 10 minutes and the rift window lasts up to 1 hour. The countdown at the top of this page converts the next opening to your local time.
What time zone does Aion 2 use?
+
The Taiwan service runs on UTC+8 (Asia/Taipei) and the Korea service on UTC+9 (Asia/Seoul). Both use the same clock schedule, so every event happens one hour apart between them. Global NA, SA, EU and Japan servers open October 5, 2026 and their server clock has not been published yet.
Why is my Aion 2 rift timer an hour off?
+
Most often you have the wrong server selected, since Korea and Taiwan run one hour apart. Some trackers also hard-code US Eastern hours, which drift by an hour whenever daylight saving changes. This page anchors every event to the server's IANA time zone, so it stays correct through DST.
What time is the daily and weekly reset in Aion 2?
+
Daily reset is at 05:00 server time. Weekly reset is Wednesday at 05:00 server time. Daily dungeon entries (7 per week) do not require daily logins and can all be cleared before Wednesday.
When do Aion 2 world bosses spawn?
+
Post-Conquest Reshanta bosses (Watcher Kaira, Executor Argo, Executor Kaira, Executor Tamasa, Executioner Dramos, Ravager Marakha, Turncoat Ducal) spawn Wednesday and Saturday at 22:30 server time and despawn 30 minutes later. Enraged Guardian Lord Nahma spawns Friday and Sunday at 22:00. Watcher Kaira also has a regular 4-hour cycle from 01:00.
When is Spacetime Rift Domination and the Abyss Rift Zone?
+
Rift Domination runs Monday, Thursday and Saturday at 20:00 and 23:00 server time with a 5-minute prep and a 15-minute match. The Abyss Rift Zone battlefield queues Tuesday and Thursday at 22:00 server time. Artifact Conquest is Wednesday and Saturday at 22:00.
When is the Aion 2 global release?
+
Aion 2 launches globally on Monday, October 5, 2026 at 6:00 AM PDT (13:00 UTC) on Steam and the PURPLE launcher, with servers for North America, South America, Europe and Japan. Founder's Pack Advance Access runs from September 30 to October 4 and opened September 30 at 7:00 AM PDT (14:00 UTC), one hour later than first planned. This page counts down to both in your local time.
Can I get Aion 2 rift alerts in Discord or on stream?
+
Yes. Paste a Discord webhook URL into the Discord alerts section and our server posts a message before each event you enable, even when this page is closed. The streamer section builds an OBS browser-source overlay URL with a live rift countdown, ticker bar, next-up cards or checklist.
How many classes are in Aion 2?
+
Aion 2 has 9 playable classes: Gladiator, Templar, Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Cleric, Chanter and Brawler. Every class is available to both factions and can be played male or female, so your class choice never locks you out of a side.
What is on the Aion 2 schedule today?
+
Spacetime Rifts run every 3 hours at 02:00, 05:00, 08:00, 11:00, 14:00, 17:00, 20:00 and 23:00 server time, the daily reset lands at 05:00 server time and the weekly reset on Wednesday at 05:00. World bosses, Shugo Festival, Dimensional Invasion and Beritra Air Raid sit on their own rotations, and the schedule on this page converts all of them into your own time zone.

Timer details
Spacetime Rift: Explained
Level 45 or higher. Elyos rifts lead into Altgard, Asmodian rifts into Verteron, with roughly nine to ten rift reference points per zone.
The portal is open for about 10 minutes. Once inside, the rift window lasts up to one hour and capacity is limited to a few hundred players per rift.
Use it for opposite-faction quests, sealed dungeons and outpost progress. NPC guards attack on sight, and switching PvP mode has a 70-minute cooldown that only works in your own territory.
Repair gear, clear bag space and load a PvP preset before you cross.
