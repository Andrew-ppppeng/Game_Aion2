About the Aion 2 Database & Interactive Map
Welcome to the most comprehensive
Aion 2 Database
. Built for the highly anticipated MMORPG sequel developed by NCSoft in Unreal Engine 5, our platform provides players with everything they need for the global release. Whether you are playing as an Elyos or Asmodian, you can explore detailed item stats, skill scaling, and drop rates sourced directly from the official client files.
Use the
Aion 2 Build Calculator
to theorycraft your perfect character setup. Choose between the 9 core classes—Templar, Gladiator, Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Elementalist, Chanter, Cleric, and Brawler. Customize your gear enhancements, Soul Inscriptions, and Stigma slots, and integrate Pet insight bonuses to maximize your PvP and PvE potential.
Beyond the database, our
Aion 2 Interactive Map
tracks every gathering node, World Boss spawn, Hidden Cube, and Rift location across all zones. Combine map tracking with our live Event Timers and Drop Rate filters to optimize your farming routes and stay ahead in the Abyss.
