Site news
October 2, 2026
10 new guides, promo codes and an event schedule with in-game art
10 new guides on the most asked questions: launch errors, monetization and membership, the Abyss, Elyos or Asmodians, auto combat and bots, world bosses, making Kinah, picking a server, account and Twitch Drops, leveling 1–45.
New «Promo codes» page: active Global codes with rewards and expiry. Codes from the official news appear there automatically.
The event schedule now shows in-game art and icons: Shugo minigames, raids, rifts, arenas and boss portraits.
Fixed «Characters» for Global: NCSoft moved character search and pages to new addresses — search and gear work again.
The Global ranking is connected to the new API and refreshes hourly. As soon as NCSoft opens the ranking on the web, it appears here automatically.
Guide comments now sit right under the article.
October 1, 2026
Global is live: the tracker for Global, quests and a streamer program
AION 2 Global launched with some trouble (queues, servers added on the fly, maintenance), yet it keeps breaking player records.
DBAion2 Tracker is optimised for Global: damage, kills, souls and drops are counted, cross-server and the cube finder work.
New in the tracker: the quest helper, a 1–45 leveling route with hints, Asmodians for now.
On the site: every Global server in the armory and ranking, news times in Moscow time.
We are launching a partner program for streamers: see the «For streamers» page.
September 29, 2026
DBAion2 Tracker, dungeon Battle Power and new guides
DBAion2 Tracker — our app for AION 2 Global: farm tracker, goals, hidden cube search, overlay and a free DPS meter. The app is in development; PvP and duels are in beta. Details are on the tracker page.
PRO plans with a launch discount for the Global release: 7, 30, 90 and 365 days. Pay with a Russian card or SBP; the DPS meter stays free.
Dungeons: required Battle Power for Conquest, Transcendence and Nightmare from the Global client — as tables and per step on dungeon pages.
Skills calculator: how many stigma shards you can buy in the Shugo Fiesta, Nightmare and Abyss shops.
Progression guide: shop limits and Battle Power tables; class guides and general guides updated.
Item, NPC and quest pages get a short «how to get» summary and similar items; a new vendors page; the About page is redone.
September 27, 2026
Living characters in the builder and item cards for your sites
Build planner: characters made from the game’s own presets — faces, hairstyles and the weapon in hand; hover a card to zoom in and watch the character breathe. Every class has its own colour and emblem.
The catalogue now holds dozens of popular Global builds by players, credited to their authors.
«Copy for embedding» on item, NPC and quest pages: link, forum BBCode, HTML and Markdown. Item links in Discord and Telegram now show a card with the icon and stats.
Item tooltips for your sites and guides: one line of code and links to dbaion2 show a pop-up card. See the «Tooltips for websites» page.
Coming soon: DBAion2 Tracker — farm, AP and PvP tracking and duels, plus a DPS meter. See the «DBAion2 Tracker and DPS meter» page.
The home page loads faster; the site rules were updated.
September 25, 2026
New home page, a Player rankings section and better filters
Home page: the eight main sections — items, interactive map, character builder, skill calculator, craft calculator, Daevanion, player rankings and guides.
New Player rankings section: characters with their gear, leaderboards by mode and the class tier list on one page.
NPC and quest filters now work like the item filters. Quests got the in-game type icons, a level filter and sorting, and every catalog can be searched by ID.
The skill calculator shows what a specialization does: its effect appears in the skill description and next to the cooldown.
Two themes remain: Nebula and Nebula Light.
And lots of small fixes.
September 24, 2026
Character builder, mobile version and beginner guides
Character builder with player builds: gear, skills and stigmas, Daevanion, looks, pets, wings and the rest. Final stats update live, variants can be compared, and a build can be shared by link.
Mobile version of the site: bottom and side menus, catalogs and calculators that work on phones.
Four new beginner guides based on the game client data.
And lots of small fixes.
September 23, 2026
Big update: English version, new themes, filters and formulas
New Nebula and Nebula Light themes. Nebula is now the default, Native stays in the style switcher. The same header and menu on every page.
English version of the site and 63 news posts in English, including translated Korean patch notes.
Items: filters by grade, race, item level and required level, a “Show more” button, a separate Pets group.
Gear calculator: total attack, PvE, boss and PvP attack, crit chance and damage using formulas players derived. Wings, wardrobe and arcana enchanting added.
Skill calculator: stigmas start at level 0, stigma points are counted correctly.
Characters: hover an equipped item to see its actual stats, mana stones and god stone.
Map: new place icons. Dungeons: pictures are no longer stretched. News: every card has its own picture.
Mirror dbaion2.online for those who cannot open the main site. The address is in the footer.
September 22, 2026
The AION 2 database is live
dbaion2.ru is open: 9,450 items, 13,494 NPCs and monsters with drops and spawn places, 1,429 quests, 315 skills, 737 recipes, 35 dungeons, 734 titles, pets — all from the game client.
Interactive map of the world and the Abyss with found marks, favourites and notes.
Calculators for gear, skills and stigmas with a skill bar, Daevanion and crafting. Share a build with a link.
Characters, rankings and a class tier list from official NCSoft data.
Search across the whole database, game news, guides and comments.
