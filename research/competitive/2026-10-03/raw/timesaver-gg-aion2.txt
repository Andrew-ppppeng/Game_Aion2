Quick answer:
AION 2 launches globally on
October 5, 2026
, free-to-play on PC via
Steam and NC's PURPLE launcher
. If you buy a
Founder's Pack
, you get in early —
5 days of Advance Access from September 30 through October 4
. Before either of those, NC is running a
two-day Launch Scale Test on September 17–18
that anyone can sign up for on the Steam page. One thing NC has
not
published yet: the exact server-open
clock time
for either the head start or launch day. Everything below is what's confirmed, what changed, and what's still a question mark — verified against NC's official channels as of August 31, 2026.
AION 2 servers and regions
AION 2 is one of the most-searched unreleased games of the year, and the search results for it are a mess — half of them are about the
original
2008 Aion, the rest are grey-market sellers guessing at dates. This is the clean version. We're tracking every official announcement from NC (NCSoft), the
official AION 2 site
, and the
Steam store page
, and we update this page the moment a date or time lands.
When does AION 2 release?
AION 2 releases worldwide on October 5, 2026.
That's the free-to-play launch — the day the servers open to everyone, no purchase required. NC confirmed the date at
Gamescom 2026 Opening Night Live on August 25, 2026
, and it's listed as the release date on the Steam store page for the game (AppID 3393110).
Two things to know about that date:
It moved.
AION 2 was originally targeting a
September 2026
window. NC pushed it to October 5 and outlets covered it explicitly as a delay. That matters because a game that has already slipped once
can
slip again — until the servers are physically up, treat the date as "currently scheduled" rather than carved in stone.
It's a single global launch date.
NC has not detailed staggered per-region unlock times. There are reported plans for separate service regions (North America, South America, Europe, Asia/Japan), but NC has
not
published a server list or per-region launch clock, so don't assume your region opens at a different hour than everyone else's — we simply don't know yet.
For context on how far along this game already is: AION 2
launched in South Korea and Taiwan on November 19, 2025
, on PC, iOS and Android. So the game itself is real, shipped, and roughly nine months into its life in Asia. What launches on October 5 is the
global build
, which NC reworked from the Korean version — the two are separate services.
When is AION 2 early access? (Founder's Pack head start)
Advance Access starts September 30, 2026, and runs through October 4
— a five-day head start before the free-to-play launch. The official Steam Founder's Pack page (AppID 4972180) states
"Advance Access Starts 30 Sep,"
and NC's press release describes it as
"five days of early access starting September 30."
Here's the important part, because it's the single most-asked question about the packs:
the only way to get early access is to buy a Founder's Pack, and the head start is the only functional thing the packs give you.
Everything else in them is cosmetic. There are three tiers, and you can buy
only one per account
:

One thing we're still verifying:
the Steam Founder's Pack page currently also lists what reads as a

not

Is early access worth it?
If you're a launch-day-hype player who wants a head start on levelling and a title, yes. If you're mostly a PvE player worried about being "stuck" on a low-population early-access server, know that veterans of the Korean and Taiwanese versions report the game is heavily instanced — as one long-time TW player put it,
"if you enjoy PvE, you will be able to play all dungeons with people from the launch server… you will not be isolated."
We can't verify that for the global build until it's live, but it's the consistent read from people who've actually played.

What time do AION 2 servers go live?
NC has not published an exact server-open time for either September 30 or October 5.
No official source has given an hour and timezone for the head-start server open or the launch-day server open as of this writing.
This is the honest answer, and it's worth saying plainly because a lot of pages will invent a time to fill the gap — don't trust those. The moment NC posts an official server-open time on the
AION 2 notice board
or the Steam page, we'll update this section with the exact hour for each major timezone (PT / ET / CEST / KST). Bookmark this page if that's what you're here for.
What is the AION 2 Launch Scale Test?
Before either launch date, NC is running a
Launch Scale Test on September 17–18, 2026
— a short, server-stress test open to everyone, not just Founder's Pack owners. You sign up through the AION 2 Steam page, download the test client during a pre-download window, and play inside the published test windows.
The exact test hours were circulating in aggregator coverage but had
not
been confirmed word-for-word from NC's official post at the time of writing, so we're not printing specific clock times for it yet — check the official Steam news post for AppID 3393110 for the confirmed windows before you plan your evening around it. What it
is
good for: a legitimate, no-cost way to actually touch the global build two-and-a-half weeks before launch, test your PC's performance, and try a class before you commit.
What platforms is AION 2 on?
The global launch is PC-only.
Steam lists Windows only — no macOS, no Linux — and you can play through
either Steam or PURPLE
, NC's own launcher and platform. Both are official; pick whichever you prefer.
A few platform questions that come up constantly:
Is AION 2 on mobile?
Not for the global launch. iOS and Android versions exist, but
only in Korea and Taiwan
. NC has not announced a Western mobile release, so there is no global "AION 2 mobile release date" — anyone claiming one is guessing.
Is AION 2 on console (PS5 / Xbox / Switch)?
No, and NC has not announced a console version.
Will my Korean or Taiwanese character transfer to global?
No transfer has been announced. The global service is separate from the KR/TW service; treat your global account as a fresh start.
Is AION 2 free to play?
Yes — AION 2 is free-to-play at launch.
No box price, no mandatory subscription just to log in and play. It's listed as Free-to-Play with in-app purchases on Steam.
The nuance — and it's a big one — is an
optional $15/month Membership.
In NC's official July 8 business-model notice, the Membership unlocks the in-game
Market
(the auction house) and the
Kina ↔ Quna Exchange
(a first-party currency conversion, similar in spirit to a WoW Token). In other words, free players can play the whole game, but the
trading economy
sits behind the Membership. That's the detail veterans argue about most — many consider $15 a fair price to unlock the market, while critics point out that free players effectively can't build up a currency reserve by selling loot. We'll have a full breakdown of the Membership and what it gates in a dedicated guide; for the release date, the headline is simple:
you do not have to pay anything to play AION 2 on October 5.
Is AION 2 the same as the original Aion?
No.
This trips up almost everyone searching, so it's worth being blunt:
AION 2, the original Aion (2008), and Aion Classic are three different games.
AION 2
(2026) is a brand-new MMORPG built in Unreal Engine 5, launching globally October 5, 2026.
Aion
(2008, "Aion: The Tower of Eternity") is the original — still running, not the same game.
Aion Classic
is NC's rolled-back re-release of that original — also not AION 2.
They share a universe (the world of Atreia, the
Elyos or Asmodian
factions, winged Daeva heroes) and a lot of the same terminology, which is exactly why the search results are so muddled. But AION 2 rebuilt the systems from the ground up. NC's own marketing describes it as
"a world 36 times larger than the original"
with
"over 200 dungeons"
— treat those as NC's claims rather than measured facts, but the point stands: this is a new game, not a patch or a remaster.
You'll also see the query
"Aion 2.0"
thrown around. That's a misspelling that collides with a 2010 patch for the
original
Aion — it is not the name of this game. If you searched "Aion 2.0 release date," you're in the right place: it's October 5, 2026.
FAQ
When does AION 2 come out?
AION 2 launches globally on October 5, 2026, free-to-play on PC via Steam and PURPLE. Founder's Pack owners get in five days early, from September 30.
What time do AION 2 servers open?
NC has not published an exact server-open time or timezone for either the September 30 head start or the October 5 launch. Don't trust any page that lists a specific hour — none has been officially confirmed yet. We'll update this page the moment NC posts one.
Is AION 2 early access worth it?
The Founder's Pack head start (September 30–October 4) is the only functional benefit of the packs — everything else is cosmetic. If a five-day levelling head start and a title matter to you, the $24.99 Standard pack delivers exactly the same early access as the $99.99 Ultimate. If you just want to play the game, waiting for the free October 5 launch costs you nothing but five days.
Is AION 2 free to play?
Yes. There's no purchase required to play. There is an optional $15/month Membership that unlocks the in-game Market and the Kina ↔ Quna currency exchange, but you can level and play the whole game without it.
Is AION 2 coming to mobile or console?
Not for the global launch. Mobile (iOS/Android) exists only in Korea and Taiwan, and no Western mobile or console version has been announced. Global AION 2 is PC-only.
Did AION 2's release date get delayed?
Yes. It was originally targeting a September 2026 window, and NC moved it to October 5, 2026, confirming the new date at Gamescom on August 25. A further slip is always possible until servers are live, so we treat the date as "currently scheduled."
Is AION 2 the same as Aion or Aion Classic?
No — it's a separate, brand-new game built in Unreal Engine 5. It shares the Atreia setting and faction names with the 2008 original, but the systems are rebuilt. "Aion 2.0" is a misspelling, not a different product.
The bottom line
Mark two dates:
September 30
if you're buying a Founder's Pack for the head start, and
October 5
for the free global launch. Sign up for the
September 17–18 Launch Scale Test
on Steam if you want to try the global build early and stress-test your PC. The one detail still missing is the exact server-open time — NC hasn't given one, and we'll update this page the instant it does.
