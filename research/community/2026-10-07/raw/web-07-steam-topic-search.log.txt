Launch FAQ

Is AION 2 subscription based? 
AION 2 is free to play with an optional monthly membership. 

 

What is the monetization of AION 2? 
AION 2 offers optional paid cosmetic items, a Battle Pass and a membership to access player-driven economy & currency exchanges. You can check out how the monetization for AION 2 Global looks in detail here[aion2.plaync.com]. 

 

Can I play this game even if I have no one to play with? 
Yes, AION 2 can be played solo or with friends!  

 

Are there any differences between the Steam and Purple versions? 
No, the in-game content is the same for both Steam and Purple users. 

 

Will any progress from the TW or KR version of the game carry over? 
No, any progress between the TW/KR and global version will not carry over.

1. The Subscription "Soft Paywall":
Aion 2 is technically Free-to-Play, but it operates much more like a subscription game. For the global release, NCSoft consolidated the original multi-subscription model down to a single monthly sub costing around $15–$16.
While you can play for free, the Auction House is locked behind this subscription. Without the ability to trade or use the market, a purely F2P account is essentially playing a "Solo Self-Found" mode. To realistically progress and participate in the end-game economy, the monthly subscription is mandatory.
2. The Core P2W Loop: Swiping for Gold:
The most prominent P2W aspect in the global version is the legal real-money trading system, which functions very similarly to the World of Warcraft Token. You can spend real money in the cash shop. You exchange those cash shop items/currency with other players for in-game currency (Kinah/Gold). You then use that in-game currency on the Auction House to buy the best gear, crafting materials, and progression items. --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
