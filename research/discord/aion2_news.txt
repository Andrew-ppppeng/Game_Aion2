> ## AION2 Team Update - Early Access: How It Will Work
> -# Reformatted from the official AION 2 EN Team Update.
> 
> Future Daevas,
> 
> Thank you for all the great questions you've asked about how Early Access servers will work. We aim to continue the trend of providing detailed information on how the service works to ensure you have a clear picture prior to launch.
> 
> ### 1. Early Access Servers and Launch Servers
> - When Early Access officially starts, the initial wave of world servers - called
Early Access Servers
- will launch. Only players who purchased any tier of Founder's Pack will be able to access them initially.
> - New servers will open at official launch, and players will be able to choose their preferred server. Players who did not purchase Early Access will also be able to join Early Access servers, if those servers remain open and available at launch.
> - A second wave of servers, called
Launch Servers
, will also open.
> 
> ### 2. Dungeon Party Matching
> - During the Early Access period, players match for parties across
all Early Access servers in a given geographical region
, so finding a group is never limited to a single server.
> - At launch, the party matching pool expands to include both Early Access and Launch servers in the same region.
> 
> ### 3. PvP and The Abyss
> - Elyos and Asmodian servers are separate, but the factions meet in
The Abyss
for action-packed PvP battles. During Early Access, Abyss matchmaking occurs between Early Access servers only.
> - After launch, servers will be matched based on how far along and how strong each server is, so players are matched against servers at a similar stage in the game.
> 
> ### 4. The Market
> - AION 2 has two Markets:
the Server Market
and
the Cross-Server Market
.
> -
The Server Market
>   - Your world's local trading hub, where you buy and sell items with other players on your server using Kinah
>   - Available from the very start of Early Access and remains available after launch
> -
The Cross-Server Market
>   - Connects players across all servers within your region, where you can also trade using Kinah with a much larger pool of buyers and sellers
>   -
Not
available at the start of Early Access or launch
>   - Will open after release, once participating servers have reached a similar stage in the game
>   - More details will be shared before the Cross-Server Market opens
> 
> ### 5. Server Transfers
> - The team understands the importance of playing together and is committed to enabling the
AION 2 Server Transfer Service
.
> - Following Early Access and Launch, the teams will evaluate server occupancy levels with the goals of alleviating congestion and streamlining related experiences. More details will be shared in a dedicated announcement in the near future.
> - In the meantime, even if you and your friends start on different servers, you can always party up for dungeons and add each other as friends.
> 
> ### Looking Ahead
> - Thank you for the questions and feedback since last week's update. The team will continue sharing details as we get closer to launch.
> 
> See you in Atreia,
>
The AION 2 Team
