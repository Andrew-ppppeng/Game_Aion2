Twitch Drops Account Linking Guide

The account you link to Twitch must be the account you're currently playing AION2 on.
  • If you're playing on Steam → Link your Steam account
  • If you're playing on NC (Purple) → Link your NC (Purple) account

Linking a different account may result in not receiving your Twitch Drops.

Even if rewards are not granted, do not separately link your account on the NC website. Doing so may unlink your existing connection, and you may become unable to access your existing character.

If you become unable to access your character, please check the FAQ for how to resolve this.

For more details on Twitch Drops, please refer to the FAQ.
