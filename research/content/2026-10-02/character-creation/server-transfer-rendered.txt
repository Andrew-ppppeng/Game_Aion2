Information on Server Transfer
2026-09-30 23:40:00


Daevas,

For those of you waiting in a queue for an existing server, or those of you who are joining just now, please consider creating your character on a recommended server. Server transfers will be available in the future starting on October 14th and you'll have a chance to move between servers within the same faction at that time. As a reminder, all instanced content including dungeons are available cross-server.


Note: When we introduce the server transfer feature, we will add restrictions to ensure Early Access players may only transfer between Early Access Servers, and not commercial launch servers.


Additionally, Server Transfers will initially be free during this period!
