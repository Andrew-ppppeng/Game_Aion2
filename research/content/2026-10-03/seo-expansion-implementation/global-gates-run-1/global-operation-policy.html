AION2 Operation Policies

1.  Sanctions/Reports, Appeals, and Customer Support

① If a User becomes aware of another User's violation of the Terms or Operations Policy, or suffers harm as a result, they may report it to the company.

(1) The procedure for reporting/sanctioning Terms violations is as follows:

•         Terms violation → (Report) → Investigation → (Pre-notification) → Sanction → Appeal → Notification of appeal result

② Reported investigations may take some time, and re-investigations may be conducted in some cases.

③ Accounts related to a reported case may be temporarily restricted during the investigation process.

④ Users may submit inquiries, requests, suggestions, and appeals regarding sanctions related to AION2 game services, account theft, reports of abusive users, etc., through the 'Report' section on the website.

(1) Methods for filing reports, inquiries, and appeals:

[Inquiry]

•         Users may submit inquiries, requests, suggestions, and appeals regarding sanctions related to AION2 game services and account matters through the 'Contact Us' section on the website.

[Report]

•         Users may submit inquiries, requests, suggestions, and appeals regarding account theft, abusive users, and other game service matters through the 'Report' section on the website.

⑤ Reports and damage investigations related to 'account theft' must be filed within 15 days from the date the incident occurred (inclusive).

⑥ Appeals must be submitted within 15 days from the date of the sanction.

 

2.  AION2 Service Operation Standards and Special Policies

①  The company will maintain neutral standpoint and not intervene in disputes between Users. However, if a dispute involves violations of the Terms or Operations Policy, or interference with normal game service operations, the company may review the matter neutrally and take necessary action.

② The company accepts reports of bugs and errors in the game service through our community sites.

③ The company may request identity verification for User accounts when necessary for account protection and service stability.

④ The company and its employees and operators may provide limited answers or no answer regarding update details, development direction, or other information that cannot be disclosed due to the nature of the game.

 

3.  Name Management Standards

① A 'name' refers to any type of identifier that a User sets within the game service to identify characters, items, groups, etc. (including nicknames, character names, legion names, etc.).

② In principle, Users are free to choose their own names.

③ Notwithstanding paragraph ②, the following types of names are prohibited, and the company may change such names without notice. Additionally, sanctions may be imposed for using such names:

(1) Names that may cause discomfort or sexual humiliation to others.

(2) Names containing profanity, vulgar language, advertising, or suggestive content.

(3) Names that are discriminatory based on race, gender, nationality, etc.

(4) Names deemed intended to criticize, insult, or defame specific individuals, groups, organizations, regions, or religions.

(5) Names that impersonate the company, its employees, or operators.

(6) Names suggesting account boosting or exchange/brokerage of game byproducts (items, accounts, legions, etc.) for cash or goods.

(7) Names that infringe or may cause harm to the trademarks or copyrights of third parties.

(8) Names prohibited under the Terms.

(9) Names that violate laws or social norms.

 

4.  Character Appearance Standards

① These standards apply to all appearances (face, hair, style, body type, etc.) set, created, or distributed by Users within the game service.

② In principle, Users are free to customize their own appearances.

③ However, the following types of appearances are prohibited, and the company may change or delete such appearances and apply related sanctions without prior notice. Creating or distributing such appearances may also be subject to sanctions:

(1) Appearances that may cause significant discomfort or sexual humiliation by general social standards or as deemed inappropriate by the NC staff.

(2) Appearances containing discriminatory elements based on race, gender, nationality, disability, age, etc.

(3) Appearances reasonably deemed intended to criticize, insult, or defame specific individuals, groups, organizations, regions, or religions.

(4) Appearances that infringe or may infringe on the trademarks, copyrights, portrait rights, or other intellectual property or personal rights of third parties

(5) Appearances that otherwise violate laws or social norms

④ In cases of rights infringement, misappropriation, or disputes between Users regarding appearances, the company will not directly intervene in the matter and bears no responsibility.

 

5.  Sanction Policy

① The types of sanctions are as follows:

(1) Permanent Integrated Account Ban: A permanent restriction on the use of all games and related services provided by the company.

(2) Permanent Game Ban: A permanent restriction on the use of the AION2 game and related services.

(3) Temporary Game Ban: A restriction on the use of the AION2 game and related services for a specified period.

(4) Warning: A temporary sanction including temporary game access restriction, chat ban, action restriction, forced character name change, forced character appearance change, operator warning, or recommendation.

(5) Item Adjustment: A sanction that deletes or modifies character information, items, or paid content related to the Terms violation.

(6) IP Block: A restriction on game or service access from a specific IP address.

② When the company applies sanctions under paragraph ①, it may recover or adjust benefits (items, experience points, etc.) related to the Terms violation. For violations where benefits are difficult to calculate (such as bug exploitation or use of unauthorized programs), the company may reset game usage data or restrict access to certain content or connection environments.

③ All responsibility for Terms violations occurring during account theft or account sharing due to the User's own fault shall be borne by both the violating party and the account holder, and sanctions may be applied to both accounts.

④ If two or more simultaneous violations are confirmed, the company may apply cumulative sanctions for each violation.

⑤ The company may also apply sanctions to Users who assisted in, conspired with, aided, or benefited from another User's Terms violation.

⑥ If a User knowingly acquires items obtained through another User's Terms violation, or unknowingly acquires such items for free, the company may recover the relevant items.

⑦ The sanction criteria and content shall follow the Sanction Table in Article 7. However, even if the criteria are met, the company may refrain from imposing a sanction or impose a lesser sanction, taking into account the specific circumstances at the time of the violation and the degree of impact on the service.

⑧ Even if not listed in the Sanction Table in Article 7, sanctions may be applied for actions that violate the Terms or applicable laws, depending on their severity.

(1)  6 months or more have elapsed since the last AION2 game login date

(2)  The AION2 paid service usage period has ended

②  In order to provide smooth Legion functionality within the game service, the Company may, upon a request from a Legion member, revoke the Legion management authority of a Legion Master character satisfying the conditions below, or transfer it to another character.

(1)  30 days or more have elapsed since the last AION2 game login date

(2)  A game usage restriction sanction of 90 days or more has been imposed

③  Where character game information within a Long-Term Inactive Account is to be deleted, the Company will give prior notice of the schedule and details of the deletion through an announcement.

④  Characters and character names deleted under the Long-Term Inactive Account Policy cannot be restored.

 

8.  Restoration Policy

①  Where game usage information (information such as items, in-game currency, points, achievements, and levels obtained by a User in the course of using the game service) is lost or altered due to a cause attributable to the Company or a technical error in the game service, restoration is possible, to the extent that it does not place undue strain on the game balance, provided that in-game records can be verified using current information and communications technology.

②  Where quest progression is not possible due to a technical error in the game service, the Company will assist where possible, within the scope in which in-game records can be verified using current information and communications technology, to allow the quest to proceed normally. Where necessary, quest items may be restored or character information may be changed.

③  A User who wishes to obtain restoration under Paragraph 1 or Paragraph 2 must apply for restoration through the customer center within 15 days from the date the game usage information was lost or altered.

④  An application for restoration under Paragraph 3 must be submitted directly by the holder of the account in which the loss or alteration of game usage information occurred, and submission by a third party on the holder's behalf is not permitted.

⑤  The Company may, based on game design or operational judgment, modify, change, or delete a User's character and item information after giving prior notice to the User, and individual restoration is not available in such cases. However, where the proviso to Article 14, Paragraph 4 of the Terms of Service applies, notice may be given after the fact. In addition, paid content may also be modified, changed, or deleted where the Company determines it necessary for game design or operational reasons (e.g., game balancing), in which case the Company shall compensate the User with another item of similar value usable within the game, and the User may not make a separate claim for damages in connection with such action by the Company.

⑥  The following losses or alterations of game usage information are not subject to restoration or compensation for damages.

(1)  Damage arising from a User's own fault or failure to become familiar with the game system, announcements, Terms of Service, and Operation Policy, etc.

(2)  Damage arising from a User's normal use of the game service, such as enhancement, crafting, or sale at a shop

(3)  Any damage arising from use of the game service in a manner not recognized by the Company, such as item trading, or the trading or sharing of accounts

(4)  Damage resulting from a device change or device reset

(5)  Loss of Legion level due to disbandment of a Legion

(6)  Damage arising from failing to accurately verify transaction details, such as item name, price, and quantity, when listing, selling, or purchasing an item on the marketplace

(7)  Damage arising from a matter between Users, such as in-game fraud, without the Company's involvement

(8)  Where damage such as account theft occurs due to a User's negligence in managing their account

-   A User may demonstrate that they have maintained account security through periodic password changes and enrollment in a security authentication service.

10.                        Policy on Managing Names of Inactive Characters

①  The Company may reset the character name within an account that satisfies any of the conditions below, in order to improve the service and ensure smooth operation of the game.

(1)  The character name of an account with no payment history among accounts with no login record for 30 days or more since the last AION2 game login date

(2)  The character name of an account subject to a Permanent Game Suspension sanction

②  Where a character name is to be reset, the Company will give prior notice of the schedule and details of the reset through an official community announcement.

③  A character name reset under the Policy on Managing Names of Inactive Characters cannot be restored.

 

11.                        Policy on Managing Names of Inactive Legions

②  Where a Legion name is to be reset, the Company will give prior notice of the schedule and details of the reset through an announcement.

③        A Legion name reset under the Policy on Managing Names of Inactive Legions cannot be restored.

 

12.                        Style Shop Policy

①  This policy is the Operation Policy applicable to the AION2 Style Shop service.

②  If a character that has registered a style in the Style Shop is deleted, any revenue arising from a style registered prior to the character's deletion (including any accrued but unsettled revenue) shall be forfeited.

③  The Company may, at any time, recover or adjust any benefit obtained by a User through circumventing a limitation in the game system's design or through violating the Terms of Service and Operation Policy.

④  This Policy takes precedence in applying sanctions to Style Shop posts, and for matters not provided for in this Policy, the AION2 Official Community Operation Policy and the PLAYNC Operation Policy shall apply.

⑤  Where a post qualifies as an objectionable Style Shop post, the post may be deleted, or settlement of revenue for the relevant style may be suspended, without prior warning.

⑥  Where customization data is to be changed, the Company will give prior notice of the schedule and details of the change through an official community announcement.
