AION 2 Global is **free-to-play at public launch**, with optional membership, premium currency, a paid Battle Pass track and Founder's Packs. Advanced Access and trading membership serve different purposes.

<h2 id="free-and-paid">What is free, and what requires payment?</h2>

Membership has a **$15/month US-dollar reference price**. Your local price, currency and taxes appear at checkout.

| System | Access and benefits |
| --- | --- |
| Base game | Free-to-play |
| Membership | $15/month reference; unlocks Market and Kina–Quna Exchange access plus gameplay benefits |
| Standard Battle Pass track | Available to all players |
| Premium Battle Pass track | Bought with Quna; additional rewards |
| Shop cosmetics | Cosmetic items have no combat stats |
| Shop consumables | Some assist play and also have gameplay acquisition routes |

You can start free and evaluate convenience or progression purchases after playing. **Market and Exchange access require active membership.** Check the final price before paying.

<h2 id="currency-and-trading">Kina, Quna, Market, and Exchange</h2>

| Term | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Kina | Gameplay-earned currency used for Market transactions |
| Quna | Premium currency for shop items and the premium pass; also participates in the Exchange |
| Market | Player item trading using Kina; active membership required |
| Exchange | Player-to-player conversion between Kina and Quna; active membership required |

The Exchange trades between players: one supplies Kina and another Quna. Quna acquired there can be spent on Quna purchases in the shop and premium pass.

Before an Exchange or Market transaction, check your membership, the available exchange offer and the item price. Exchange rates depend on player offers.

<h2 id="quna-use-and-budget">What Quna does, how to get it and how much to spend</h2>

**Quna is premium currency.** Use it for Quna-priced shop items, the premium Battle Pass track or buying Kina through the player Exchange. You can obtain it through a Quna purchase or by selling Kina to another player on the Exchange. **Exchange access requires active membership.** Direct player-to-player item trading is disabled; use the Market for player item trades.

There is no purchase required to enter the public-launch base game. For a spending plan, separate these costs:

- **Access:** public launch is free; an eligible pack is needed only for the remaining Advanced Access window.
- **Trading:** membership unlocks Market and Exchange access; the US-dollar reference is $15 per month.
- **Quna purchases:** compare the offered Quna amount with the final local-currency checkout price. A player Exchange rate is not the cash price of a Quna pack.
- **Progression:** premium-pass rewards include upgrade materials and currency; cosmetics have no combat stats.

Buy for a specific item or benefit instead of budgeting an assumed amount to “win.” Membership provides priority queue access for accounts in good standing when servers are full, but it does not guarantee immediate login.

<h2 id="founder-packs">Founder's Packs: US Steam prices</h2>

These are **US Steam prices in USD**. Open the [Founder's Pack store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4972180/) for your regional offer.

| Tier | US Steam price | Main contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $24.99 | Advanced Access, supply chest, exclusive title, 30-day Special Quai Membership |
| Deluxe | $49.99 | Standard contents plus Ascended Daeva armor appearance and Eternal Sun weapon appearance |
| Ultimate | $99.99 | Deluxe contents plus styling chest, Moonlit Aria appearance, Black Dragon pet, and Blazing Sun wings |

All three packs include the five-day Advanced Access window and 30 days of Special Quai Membership. Membership benefits include remote storage, Market interaction and additional dungeon reward selections.

**Advanced Access ends October 5 at 05:00 UTC.** Buying late does not extend the window. Public access is scheduled for 13:00 UTC after maintenance. Compare the remaining time with the cosmetics and membership you want.

<h2 id="founder-cosmetic-access">Founder cosmetics and one-time rewards</h2>

The Founder cosmetic change will let your account use its included cosmetics on **all characters across all servers**. Implementation is still pending and is planned for after Advanced Access; do not assume a newly created character can already claim them.

The eligible cosmetics depend on the purchased tier: Standard's title, Deluxe's armor and weapon skins, and Ultimate's additional armor skin, Black Dragon pet and Blazing Sun wings. **The 30-day membership, Supply Chest, Styling Chest and their contents remain one-time rewards.**

If this changes the tier you want, PURPLE supports an upgrade to a higher pack. For Steam, the announced route is to buy the higher tier on the same account and request a refund for the lower pack through support; the exact process is still pending. Check the process with [support](https://help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global) before paying twice.

<h2 id="passes-and-progression">Battle Pass, cosmetics, and progression</h2>

The Battle Pass is **per character**, with a free track and a Quna premium track. Rewards include cosmetics, upgrade materials and in-game currencies. Choose your character before buying the premium track.

Shop cosmetics have no combat stats. Other paid options include materials, consumables, trading access and convenience, so compare their contents with your play goals.

Use [builds](/builds) to check your material needs. Collect additional rewards through [Twitch Drops](/twitch-drops) and the [Global coupon](/code).

<h2 id="purchase-checklist">Before paying, and if a purchase fails</h2>

1. Confirm the storefront region, currency, and final amount.
2. Check the Steam or PURPLE account containing the character you intend to use.
3. Read the offer's duration, included membership, and character restrictions.
4. For a pass, confirm the intended character and current reward track.
5. For an Exchange or Market plan, check membership and the live player offer.
6. Keep the receipt and exact error if the purchase or delivery fails.

Restart the client to apply the fix for some purchase and Advanced Access errors. If the problem persists, contact [support](https://help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global) with the platform, receipt and error.
