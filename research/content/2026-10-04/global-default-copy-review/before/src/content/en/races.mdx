**Elyos and Asmodians have different home worlds and campaign routes.** Elyos explore Verteron; Asmodians explore Altgard. Your faction also determines your home-server list and your side in the Abyss and Spacetime Rifts. Dungeon parties can include players from both factions and different servers.

<h2 id="elyos-or-asmodians">Elyos or Asmodians: what changes?</h2>

| Choice | Game content |
| --- | --- |
| Elyos | Verteron campaign and Elyos home servers; Asmodians are the opposing faction in cross-faction PvP |
| Asmodians | Altgard campaign and Asmodian home servers; Elyos are the opposing faction in cross-faction PvP |
| [Class](/classes) | Choose your combat role separately from faction, from the eight Global classes |

<h2 id="shared-dungeons">Can Elyos and Asmodians run dungeons together?</h2>

**Yes. Global dungeon parties support players from the opposite faction and different servers.** Leave **My faction only** unchecked when opening a party to both factions; enable it when you want a same-faction party.

| Content with friends | Faction and server requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Home-world questing and exploration | Same region, faction and home server |
| Instanced dungeon parties | Can include both factions and players from different servers |
| Abyss and Spacetime Rift battles | Faction determines your side and the opposing server's players |

<h2 id="choose-with-friends">Faction and home-server selection</h2>

Elyos and Asmodians have separate home-server lists. Agree on the **region, faction and exact server name** for shared world questing: for example, Europe, Elyos, Siel. Server names can repeat across regions.

An opposing server paired with yours is a war opponent, rather than another home server for shared campaign progress. The [server guide](/server) lists regions and factions; [character creation](/character-creation) covers creating your character.

<h2 id="cross-faction-opponents">Abyss and Spacetime Rift opponents</h2>

In the **Abyss**, Elyos and Asmodians fight on opposing sides. In **Spacetime Rifts**, you travel to the paired enemy faction's territory: an Elyos character visits the Asmodian side, and an Asmodian character visits the Elyos side.

Each Elyos server is paired with an Asmodian server for these activities. The pairings can change, so the opposing server's player group can change while your character keeps its own faction and home server. See [PvP](/pvp) for the combat modes and [Spacetime Rifts](/spacetime-rift) for entering enemy territory.

<h2 id="changing-faction">Does a server transfer change your faction?</h2>

**Global server transfers keep your faction.** Transfers begin on **October 14, 2026**; initially, Early Access characters can transfer only to Early Access servers. An Elyos character remains Elyos, and an Asmodian character remains Asmodian. See [server transfer](/server-transfer) for the transfer conditions.
