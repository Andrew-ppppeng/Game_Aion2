Global server transfers are **planned to open October 14, 2026**, initially free and within the same faction. Advanced Access characters can initially move only between Advanced Access servers.

<h2 id="confirmed-transfer-rules">Global transfer rules</h2>

| Question | Transfer condition |
| --- | --- |
| When are transfers planned to start? | October 14, 2026 |
| Can a move change faction? | Transfers stay within the same faction |
| Can Early Access players move to public-launch servers immediately? | Initially, Early Access players may transfer only between Early Access servers |
| What is the initial charge? | Initially free |
| Do friends need a transfer for shared dungeons? | Dungeons support cross-server play |

The free opening offer retains the same-faction and Advanced Access restrictions. Choose an eligible destination before planning a move.

<h2 id="choose-a-destination">Choose a destination before the opening</h2>

Agree on the exact region, faction and server name with your group using the [server list](/server). Your opposing-faction partner is a PvP matchup, not a same-faction transfer destination.

For shared open-world play, aim for the same home server. Dungeons and other instances support cross-server play, so you can group for those without moving your character.

<h2 id="prepare-your-information">Prepare the information you will need</h2>

1. Record the character’s current region, faction and exact server name.
2. Record the destination your group agreed on, including its faction and access pool.
3. Reopen the transfer announcement and look for a later revision or a new eligibility notice.
4. Keep a note of the reason for moving: playing on the same home server, joining an organization, or another specific need.
5. At opening, read the actual destination selector and confirmation before committing.

Choose a destination offered in the transfer selector. If the character list is unexpected, check the [account and client identity](/steam) containing the character you want to move.

<h2 id="unannounced-details">Before confirming a transfer</h2>

Before confirming, read the transfer conditions for your character: destination eligibility, price, name conflicts, cooldown and how guild membership, mail and Market listings are handled. Apply the Global conditions to your move.

<h2 id="check-at-opening">What to check when transfers open</h2>

At opening, check the [service announcements](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/list) and [maintenance information](/maintenance). Wait until the transfer service is available for your character.

Check the destination and final charge on the confirmation screen before accepting. After the move, log in to the destination and check that you selected the intended character.
