Skill bar 5 rows of 12 slots, like the in-game skill window (key K): bottom row — main bar, top rows — chains Clear bar Copy link Save build Drag an active skill or stigma from the window into a slot. On a phone: pick a skill, then tap a slot. Slots can be dragged onto each other; the circle on the left turns auto-use on, the cross on the right removes the skill. AION 2 skill and stigma calculator The AION 2 skill calculator shows the full skill tree of every class: active and passive skills, their levels, MP cost, cooldown, damage and specialisations. Skill points are counted automatically from your level and their sources — quests, dungeons, the Shugo game and level-up bonuses. A separate Stigma tab covers stigma skills: the stigma cap in the current patch is level 20, and properties unlock at levels 5, 10, 15 and 20. Chain skills are grouped so you can see the order in which they are used. Build your character, save the link and share it — the build opens exactly as you left it. The cost of every skill level comes from the game client tables.
