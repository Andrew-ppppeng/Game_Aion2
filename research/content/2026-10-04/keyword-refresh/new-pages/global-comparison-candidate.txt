Watch out
Don't spend scarce materials on an Asian-release stat priority or "best in slot" list on day one. Global characters have less total stat (halved Pantheon, weaker boards and titles, smaller enhancement bonuses), so the stat weights behind those lists don't carry over.

What carries over from the Asian release?
Mechanics, formulas, class kits and dungeon strategy carry over; item levels, rewards, growth numbers and the economy are Global's own.

Why do some guides quote numbers 100 times bigger?
Asian-release guides use two scales: Combat Power, which runs to hundreds of thousands, and item level, which runs to thousands. A number 100 times off is on the other scale.
