How we read the game files
About the AION 2 item database
This AION 2 item database is read straight from the game files of the global client, not from the Korean version, so every name, stat and drop chance matches what players in North America and Europe see. It covers 6,617 items: 2,024 weapons, armor pieces, accessories and Arcana cards, 457 crafting and gathering materials and 4,136 consumables, boxes and unlock items in 59 categories. Each item page shows the fixed stats, random stat rolls, enhancement rates, the exact monsters that drop it and every other source the game lists.

AION 2 items FAQ
How many items are in AION 2?
The global client lists 6,617 released items: 2,024 pieces of gear, 457 materials and 4,136 consumables, boxes and unlock items. Placeholder and unreleased rows are left out.
What are the item grades in AION 2?
From lowest to highest the grades are Common, Rare, Epic, Unique, Heroic, Mythic, and Special. Each grade has its own colour in the game, and the same colours are used across this database.
How do I find where an item drops in AION 2?
Search the item above and open its page. The how-to-get section lists every monster that drops it with the exact chance and the kills you need for a 90% chance, plus dungeons, quests, recipes and shops.
Is this data from the global or the Korean version of AION 2?
From the global version. The Korean and Taiwanese servers are months ahead, so their items, numbers and names differ; every value here comes from the global client that North American and European players download.
