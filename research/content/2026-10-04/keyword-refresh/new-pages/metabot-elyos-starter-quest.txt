How do I start The Power in the Lake?
The Power in the Lake unlocks at level 1 for Elyos characters and is recommended around level 5. Daminu (Elim) gives the quest in Poeta.
Where do I turn in The Power in the Lake?
Turn The Power in the Lake in to Asahr in Poeta.
What does The Power in the Lake reward?
The Power in the Lake gives 2,959 EXP and Kina (Bound) and Lesser Daeva Wings (Bound); the reward table lists every item and amount.
