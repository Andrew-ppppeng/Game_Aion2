About wings in AION 2
Wings are a signature of every Daeva in AION 2 and also add stats. The global client lists 28 wings in 5 grades; higher grades carry larger bonuses such as HP, defense and status-effect resistance. Some are faction-specific, which the faction column shows.

AION 2 wings FAQ
How many wings are in AION 2?
The global client lists 28 wings in 5 grades: Special, Unique, Epic, and Rare.
Do wings give stats?
Yes. Each pair adds its own stats, listed in the tables above; higher grades add more.
What are the rarest wings in AION 2?
The top grade is Special, with 3 wings: Blazing Sun Wings, Punk Bunny Bag Wings, and Void Rending Wings.
Do wings give stats when they are not equipped?
Yes. All 28 wings have an owned effect: its stats apply while the wing is in your collection and grow with its level, up to +10.
How much does it cost to upgrade wings?
Upgrade costs depend on the wing's grade. Expected totals from +0 to +10: Rare: 3,494,000 Kina (All), 34 Sync Stone (Unique) (Bound), and 13,000 Enhance Stone, Epic: 5,960,000 Kina (All), 53 Sync Stone (Unique) (Bound), and 23,000 Enhance Stone, and Unique: 16,741,000 Kina (All), 137 Sync Stone (Unique) (Bound), and 66,000 Enhance Stone.

Yes. Tick the box next to any of the 28 entries on this page (or use the toggle on its own page) and the progress bar above counts it.
