<h2 id="start-global">Start with the right Global account and server</h2>

AION 2 Global is a PC MMORPG available through Steam and PURPLE. As of October 2, 2026, eligible players can enter Advance Access; the public free-to-play launch is planned for October 5. These dates and platforms come from [NC's October 1 launch announcement](https://about.ncsoft.com/news/article/A2_update_20261001).

For a first session, use this order:

1. Follow the [official-client download guide](/download) and finish updates.
2. Agree on your region, faction and exact [server](/server) with friends.
3. Choose a [class](/classes) for the role you enjoy, rather than a ranking copied from another region.
4. Finish [character creation](/character-creation), then check movement, camera and combat bindings.
5. Start the main story and revisit new skill options as the game introduces them.

Global has eight launch classes in the [official introduction](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/about/index). Later KR/TW content should not determine your Global class or progression choices.

**Before the first quest:** inspect potion auto-use, loot selection and HUD readability. [A Global Launch Scale Test player](https://space4games.com/en/games-en/aion-2-beginner-guide/) shows **Combat → Auto Use**, **Combat → Looting** and **HUD Edit**. These are test-client labels; use your current equivalent and read its tooltip rather than copying an unverified threshold or automatic disposal filter.

<h2 id="main-story-first">Use the main story as your progression spine</h2>

Keep progressing the main story rather than abandoning it when your level looks high enough. A [TW player's beginner retrospective](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C73KG9MvqUk) explains that story completion opens further progression and provides a transitional weapon. That is useful player guidance; exact Global item names and reward values should be read from your quest panel.

When the story stops, inspect the actual requirement before grinding:

| Blocker | Check | Useful next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quest is not available | Previous quest completion and required character level | Reopen the journal and finish the missing step |
| Ascension/progression requirement | Its displayed gauge or objective | Complete nearby eligible regional content shown by your client |
| Boss kills you | Avoidable attacks, weapon, skills and consumables | Learn the mechanic, then make a targeted improvement |
| Dungeon entry is locked | Its current level/item requirement and unlock quest | Complete that requirement rather than raising an unrelated number |

The [leveling guide](/leveling) separates Elyos and Asmodian routes and marks the TW speedrun checkpoints as a reference, not a guaranteed Global itinerary.

**Example: the story destination is visible, but you cannot continue.** Open that objective and copy its requirement into a short note: “Current objective / required level or progression / next accepted action.” If it asks for Ascension progress, inspect the accepted regional objectives. If it asks for an item requirement, compare your equipment. After satisfying the displayed gate, return to the same campaign objective. This is a troubleshooting method, not an invented Global quest chain.

<h2 id="skills-and-gear">Improve skills and gear with a clear purpose</h2>

Character level, item level/gear score and Combat Power describe different parts of progression. Raising equipment alone does not complete a quest or grant character experience. The [TW retrospective](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C73KG9MvqUk) recommends first meeting a content's gear-entry requirement, then improving the broader systems that make a character effective.

- Equip useful quest rewards as you progress; compare their actual stats instead of chasing rarity alone.
- Read your most-used damage, defensive and support skills when new options unlock.
- If your client offers an in-built skill-combination feature, configure it for your available skills and bindings. Do not copy a different class's sequence blindly.
- Spend upgrade materials to solve a clear weakness or entry requirement; expect to replace early equipment.
- Read the current enhancement confirmation, especially when an item has a distinct failure rule.

For specific skill choices, use [builds and skills](/builds). The [player systems overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tn7WKMXt5Wo) covers skills and Stigma, but also warns that Global settings were not all confirmed when it was recorded.

<h2 id="keep-materials">Keep materials until you know their use</h2>

Avoid selling or deleting unfamiliar materials just because they resemble ordinary loot. The [TW beginner video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C73KG9MvqUk) gives examples of exploration rewards, horns, leather and scales feeding later progression. Their exact Global recipes and quantities can differ.

Before clearing your bag, inspect the tooltip, crafting recipe or progression screen. Put materials with an unclear future use into storage while you investigate. If using quick-storage or automatic extraction, review the selection rules first; protect equipment required by a quest or supply request. An old video's recommended rarity filters are not a universal setting for your current character.

For gathering, start with a material your displayed recipe actually needs. A profitable-looking old route is less useful than a short route for a real upgrade; see [gathering](/gathering).

<h2 id="exploration-checklist">Make exploration part of ordinary travel</h2>

The [player systems overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tn7WKMXt5Wo) describes sealed dungeons, strongholds, travel points and feather/Monolith progression in KR/TW. Use them as a checklist of systems to inspect when your Global client introduces them, not a promise of identical reward totals.

| When you encounter it | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| New travel point | Whether it is activated and usable for return travel |
| Nearby feather or exploration collectible | Current collection progress and the associated reward screen |
| Sealed dungeon | Required level, objective and one-time progression rewards |
| Stronghold | Completion state and the equipment materials shown |
| Pet or other persistent growth system | Available unlocks while doing your regular quests |

Take nearby collectibles while leveling, then schedule a dedicated cleanup session. Use the [map guide](/map) to enable relevant layers and avoid confusing a cave point with one on the surface. Opposite-faction exploration can expose you to [PvP](/pvp); it is not an essential first-session errand.

<h2 id="next-session">Plan the next session and the move to level 45</h2>

<GuideVisual id="workflow" />

The [official Global dungeon showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_TjiOh33a4) confirms level 45 as the launch maximum. Finish your remaining story objectives and check the content unlocks at that milestone before starting a repeatable routine.

At the start of each session, check official notices, one clear progression goal and any limited reward counters visible in your client. Then pursue that goal: a story block, a skill improvement, a collection area or your next dungeon. The player overview's daily duties and weekly contracts are useful screens to inspect, but this guide does not assign unverified Global reset hours or account limits.

Claim [official codes](/code) and [Twitch Drops](/twitch-drops) through the appropriate account when available. End by checking bag space, your next quest and the requirement for your next activity. That gives you an actionable starting point without trying to finish every system at once.
