<h2 id="global-leveling">Level toward Global's launch cap of 45</h2>

**Global's launch maximum is level 45**, confirmed by the [official Dungeon Live Gameplay Showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_TjiOh33a4). Follow the main story and complete the requirements that actually block its next step. This guide gives separate faction priorities and a clearly labeled TW route reference; it does not promise a universal speedrun time.

Later guides that continue to 50 may be correct for a different region. NC's [July 6 Chapter 1 announcement](https://about.ncsoft.com/en/news/article/aion2_update_260706) describes the later KR/TW expansion and level increase. That is not the endpoint for Global launch leveling.

Keep three goals separate: **character level** advances with experience, **item level/gear score** can gate equipment content, and **Combat Power** reflects wider character strength. An equipment upgrade may help you beat a quest boss without increasing your character's experience bar.

**First route check:** select your current campaign objective and compare its destination with the zone displayed by your character. [A Global Launch Scale Test player](https://space4games.com/en/games-en/aion-2-beginner-guide/) reports a gold-marked main quest and automatic travel after selecting a quest. Use the current tracking/navigation controls if available, then stop to inspect a gate or dangerous terrain. The test observation does not establish a permanent key binding or identical automation for every live quest.

<h2 id="elyos-route">Elyos: main story first, nearby requirements second</h2>

Use your Elyos journal's current campaign chain and its Verteron destinations. The [official world introduction](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/about/index) confirms landmarks including Cantas Valley and Dawn Legion Base, but does not publish a complete Global task-by-task itinerary.

1. Finish the introductory story and follow the next campaign objective rather than clearing every optional marker.
2. In Verteron, take nearby regional quests when they satisfy a displayed progression requirement or offer a useful reward.
3. When Ascension blocks the story, inspect its objective/gauge, complete eligible nearby content and return to the campaign.
4. Equip useful weapon and accessory rewards, then review newly available skill options.
5. After reaching 45, finish remaining story objectives before treating the character as ready for every endgame activity.

A [TW Elyos Ranger walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5UXLn7XDF8) demonstrates the story-led approach. Its checkpoints below are **TW player reference points**, not verified Global quest names or mandatory levels.

| TW walkthrough checkpoint | What the author checks | How to use it on Global |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Around 12 and 14 | Nearby regional side quests | Inspect nearby rewards and current progression needs |
| Around 22 | Ascension progress interrupts the story | Check your displayed requirement; do not copy subtitle quest names |
| Around 29 and 32 | Regional quests, accessories and another progression gate | Review newly available nearby content before grinding |
| Around 36 | Additional nearby side quests | Compare the current journal and useful rewards |
| Toward 45 | Continue the story | Finish the Global campaign objectives available to you |

<h2 id="asmodian-route">Asmodians: follow the Altgard campaign</h2>

Follow the Asmodian campaign presented by your own journal. Global's [official introduction](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/about/index) places Asmodian landmarks in Altgard, including Safe Haven, Moslan Forest and the Nornir Assembly. Elyos quest names and destinations are not substitutes for this route.

- During the opening, complete the story's introductions and access requirements in order.
- In Altgard, follow your active campaign destination and combine nearby eligible side objectives when a gate appears.
- Check the progression/Ascension panel at a story block and use the content your current client accepts for it.
- Inspect sealed dungeons or regional objectives that provide a useful unlock or reward; avoid a distant detour solely because it was efficient in an Elyos video.
- Approaching 45, finish the remaining campaign and inspect the entry panel for your next activity.

For a map-based companion, [Aion2Hub's leveling directory](https://aion2hub.com/leveling) has separate faction pages and labels its 45–50 extension KR/TW. It is a community route resource; confirm any numeric gear gates against your Global client.

<h2 id="dungeon-checkpoints">Use official dungeon milestones as checkpoints</h2>

The [official Global showcase](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_TjiOh33a4) outlines these Exploration progression landmarks:

| Character level | Showcase landmark | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | First Exploration expedition | Check its actual unlock quest and entry requirements |
| 28 | Urugugu Canyon | Learn the encounter while progressing the story |
| 35 | Fire Temple | Review skills and mechanics before attempting it |
| 45 | Further expeditions and maximum-level progression | Complete remaining story and check the available mode |

These are official presentation landmarks, not a promise that level alone opens every menu. The client's quest and equipment requirements still decide entry. Do not repeatedly run an activity for leveling without checking that it supplies the experience or progression you need.

**Worked entry check from a Global test:** [the same firsthand report](https://space4games.com/en/games-en/aion-2-beginner-guide/) lists **Krao Cave at character level 20 and item level 200** in the dungeon window, while its level guide recommends level 22. That example explains why a recommended milestone and an actual entry requirement can differ. If your character is level 20 but entry is denied, open the current dungeon panel and check the unlock quest and item requirement separately. The number 200 is a reported test value, not a new universal live requirement.

<h2 id="leveling-blockers">Troubleshoot a stalled route</h2>

| Symptom | Check first | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No next story quest | Journal, preceding turn-in and required level | Complete the missing objective or nearby eligible experience content |
| Progression gauge is short | Current Ascension instructions | Use accepted regional content instead of unrelated monster grinding |
| A boss takes too long | Weapon, active skills and missed mechanics | Equip relevant rewards and adjust your [build](/builds) |
| Dungeon entry is denied | Current quest, level and item requirement | Satisfy the listed gate |
| Travel consumes most of the session | Activated travel points and terrain | Use the [map](/map), group nearby objectives and review flight bindings |

The [TW route author](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5UXLn7XDF8) emphasizes damage skills, updated quest gear and nearby feathers. Those priorities can guide inspection; his run time and precise upgrade assumptions are not guarantees for every Global class.

<h2 id="after-leveling">What to do after reaching 45</h2>

Finish the remaining story, inspect available progression tasks and identify the next actual entry requirement. Clear accessible unfinished exploration for persistent rewards rather than assuming character level finishes every growth system. The [player systems overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tn7WKMXt5Wo) explains sealed dungeons, strongholds and collectibles in KR/TW; verify their Global reward screens before planning a full circuit.

Choose one next goal: a stronger skill setup, an unfinished collection area, a dungeon unlock or a needed equipment improvement. Use [beginner priorities](/guide) and [builds](/builds) to organize that transition. A completed 1–45 route is the beginning of ongoing character progression, not a guarantee that every higher-mode boss is ready to be fought.
