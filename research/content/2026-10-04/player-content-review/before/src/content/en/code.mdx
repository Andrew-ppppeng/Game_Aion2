<h2 id="redeem-coupon">How to redeem the coupon</h2>

NC gives this in-game route: **Settings → Miscellaneous → Account → Enter Coupon**. [Official instructions](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abd5fe5fa7da41c727d63c6).

1. Launch the Global client and sign in with the account you actually play.
2. Open Settings, then Miscellaneous and Account.
3. Select Enter Coupon.
4. Copy the coupon from the card above. Check for an extra space before or after it.
5. Select Confirm and read the client's result before trying again.

During Advanced Access, having the client installed does not by itself grant access to the game. If you cannot reach the redemption menu, check your launch entitlement and the [Steam guide](/steam). Public Global access is scheduled for October 5 at 13:00 UTC; that launch time is a separate deadline from the coupon's expiry. [Official access schedule](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6ab85cc646be804931c31335).

<h2 id="redemption-problems">If the coupon is rejected</h2>

Use the error shown by the client to narrow the problem before repeating the same action.

| Check | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Correct region | Confirm you are playing Global; a KR or TW promotion is not evidence of Global eligibility. |
| Correct text | Compare the entire coupon with the card, including its final characters; remove copied spaces. |
| Announced expiry | Check the expiry shown above and the official notice, including its time zone. |
| Previous use | Check whether this account already redeemed it; the allowance is shared across servers. |
| Login or service problem | Read the latest [maintenance information](/maintenance) before treating a failed login as an invalid coupon. |

These checks do not prove that a reward has been delivered. If the result remains unclear, contact [AION 2 support](https://help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global) with the exact error, region, account login method, and approximate attempt time. Do not publish your password or verification code with an error report.
