<h2 id="start-gathering">Start gathering without importing another region's unlock rule</h2>

A **Global Advance Access player report** documents gathering below character level 45. [S4G's Kevin Link](https://space4games.com/en/games-en/aion-2-essence-extraction-guide/) says he began around level 10–11 after leaving Safe Haven as an Asmodian; his Spiritmaster was level 12 while gathering was Novice 13. This is a reported Global example, not an official universal unlock rule. Check the requirement shown by your own Global client before planning a long loop.

Why do some guides say you must be level 45? NC's [January 28 anti-bot announcement](https://about.ncsoft.com/en/news/article/aion2_update_260128) increased the gathering unlock level to 45 in the then-operating Korean version. That regional change is real, but it is not proof of the Global rule. Do not stop your Global leveling solely because a KR tutorial gives that number.

Start with an accessible node and a material you actually need. Check its tooltip, gather it, and inspect which experience or proficiency bar changes. This short test is more useful than buying materials or spending specialization points based on a conflicting guide.

<h2 id="experience-specialization">Separate gathering experience, specialization and crafting</h2>

For a first interaction, [the Global report](https://space4games.com/en/games-en/aion-2-essence-extraction-guide/) describes competing **Success** and **Failure** gauges. Check gathering progress under **Essence Extraction**; **Item Extraction** dismantles equipment and is a different menu. Watch the actual result instead of assuming every attempt yields a material.

The [Mr4KTV gathering walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g5wt6NOSjVU) demonstrates different resource types contributing to gathering experience in the author's KR/TW version. It separately discusses functional gathering skills and material-specific specialization. These are player observations, not a verified Global point allocation.

| System | Question to answer in your client |
| --- | --- |
| Gathering experience/proficiency | Does this node advance the same displayed bar as another resource? |
| General gathering skills | Does the tooltip affect success, speed or another gathering cost? |
| Resource specialization | Does it reveal nodes, improve yield or affect quality for your target material? |
| Crafting profession | Which recipe consumes the material, and what crafting requirements does it have? |

Do not treat a crafting profession's level as the gathering requirement for every ingredient. The [player systems overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tn7WKMXt5Wo) also separates gathering functions such as efficiency and specialization. Read each current tooltip before spending points.

<h2 id="choose-materials">Choose materials and skills for a real goal</h2>

<GuideVisual id="workflow" />

1. Open the recipe or upgrade screen you want to complete and note the missing material names and quantities.
2. Find those materials in your current region's map and check whether your character can gather them.
3. Inspect the relevant gathering skill before investing. Prefer an improvement you can use repeatedly over one for a resource you do not need.
4. Run a short trial and compare material yield, failures and time spent traveling.
5. Recheck the plan when you change crafting goals or unlock a new resource tier.

Mr4KTV particularly recommends Odyle in his version because of its repeated uses, including nodes encountered after dungeon content. That is a **KR/TW player recommendation**; check your Global recipes and dungeon rewards before treating it as the best resource for every character. His exact skill-point setup is deliberately not reproduced as a Global build.

<h2 id="gathering-routes">Find nodes and build a compact route</h2>

Use [Aion2T's map](https://aion2t.com/map) to select a zone and narrow resource markers. For high platforms or caves, [Aion2Maps](https://www.aion2maps.com) can provide height context; its [creator](https://www.reddit.com/r/Aion2/comments/1wtsfhn/3d_interactive_voxel_maps_for_aion_2_generated/) says the dataset was generated from Global client coordinates. Neither tool tells you that a particular node is currently available.

Build your first route around a small cluster near a familiar landmark:

- Record your faction, zone and starting point so you can repeat the same test.
- Pick one material, keep unrelated layers hidden and check terrain access.
- Note the node's displayed requirement before interacting.
- Skip missing or blocked nodes on the first pass; do not assume a fixed respawn time.
- End the test after a chosen interval and count useful materials, not just interactions.

Use the [map guide](/map) when a marker appears empty or on the wrong vertical layer. A route that requires opposite-faction travel should also account for [rift/PvP risk](/spacetime-rift).

<GuideVisual id="topic" />

**Run a repeatable ten-minute trial:** choose one required material, your faction's zone and three nearby accessible candidate stops. Start a timer at a known landmark. For each stop, note the node requirement, whether an attempt succeeds and the useful material obtained. At ten minutes, record total useful yield and time spent traveling. This is a suggested measurement method, not a tested yield or a guaranteed farming route.

<h2 id="promotion-and-value">Resolve promotion blocks and decide whether to gather or buy</h2>

**A reported Global Asmodian promotion example:** [S4G's author](https://space4games.com/en/games-en/aion-2-essence-extraction-guide/) reached **Novice 50**, then followed the upgrade quest to **Alzirr in Safe Haven**. He reports obtaining the required **Splendent Ruby (Bound)** from a Ruby node in **Calderon Canyon** at character level 20. He distinguishes this quest item from **Radiant Ruby Gemstone**, a crafting material. Check your current quest tooltip and turn-in requirement before buying a similarly named gem; the wiki has not repeated that in-game turn-in.

The KR/TW walkthrough shows a promotion step after its basic gathering stage. If your bar stops advancing, inspect your gathering panel and the available quest instructions rather than assuming the profession is broken. The creator is uncertain about the exact promotion item name in his commentary, so this guide does not present that subtitle spelling as an official Global quest requirement.

| Situation | Practical response |
| --- | --- |
| Experience stops increasing | Check whether the current stage is capped or a promotion quest is available |
| Frequent failures | Compare your proficiency and the node requirement; inspect relevant success-related skills |
| You need one small batch immediately | Compare the actual purchase price with the time to obtain that batch |
| You will need the material repeatedly | Build a repeatable route and invest only in skills that serve it |
| Someone claims a rare-material percentage | Ask for the region, sample size and game version; an anecdote is not an official drop rate |

Gathering may save currency, while buying may save time. Compare the market options available to your account with a timed test of your own route; neither guarantees profit. Keep crafting materials until you know their use, and revisit the [beginner guide](/guide) for inventory and progression priorities.
