<h2 id="steam-account-help">Steam login, rewards, and launch problems</h2>

1. Install or update the official AION 2 application through Steam.
2. Launch using the Steam identity that owns your Advanced Access entitlement, if playing before public opening.
3. If an entitlement error matches NC's known issue, restart the client to apply the published fix.
4. For Twitch, select the Steam login identity when linking the game account.
5. Record the exact error and contact official support if the issue remains unresolved.

NC published a no-downtime fix for some access-recognition and purchase issues; restarting applies that client update. It did not state that every account-linking case was resolved. [Official fix notice](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abe2cf8a7ca1a15cf46bcbe).

The October 2 [account linking guide](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abf40d99ed1202b9b8fb4fb) says to link Steam for Steam players and NC for PURPLE players. Do not create an additional NC connection as a missing-reward workaround: NC warns it can remove your existing link and affect access to your character. See [Twitch Drops](/twitch-drops) for the claim checks, or contact [support](https://help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global).

For SteamCharts, SteamDB and concurrent-player searches, use the [player-count guide](/player-count). It labels the observed Steam snapshot and explains why Steam concurrency does not equal every Global player.
