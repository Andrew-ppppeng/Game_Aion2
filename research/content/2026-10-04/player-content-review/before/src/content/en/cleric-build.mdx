For party PvE, put **a direct heal, cleanse, and resurrection on separate reachable controls**, then use Debilitating Mark → Chains of Torment → Earth's Punishment and available Condemnation attacks. Equip party protection and Noble Aura when the group needs their benefits; exchange damage for healing if recovery is the problem.

<h2 id="choose-your-cleric-setup">Choose the setup for today's content</h2>

| Situation | Starting emphasis | Adjustment trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo questing | Reliable damage with self-recovery available | Add recovery when pulls interrupt your progress |
| A regular dungeon party | Healing, cleanse, resurrection, and useful boss debuffs | Add an emergency heal if routine damage becomes difficult |
| Party with a Chanter | Check the Chanter's buff coverage before duplicating protection | Swap only after reading the actual effects and agreeing coverage |
| Party without a Chanter | Consider the party protection option demonstrated in the guides | Preserve it if its protection or damage benefit is needed |
| Learning a high-pressure encounter | Give healing and defensive reactions more room | Trade a marginal damage option for the tool that addresses the wipe |

BoredAF describes maintaining debuffs while recovering from mistakes; aLuckyRO changes the setup according to Chanter presence. Do not read this as permission to ignore injured allies. A missed lethal mechanic may require resurrection, while ordinary damage or a removable debuff calls for a different response.

<h2 id="starting-stigma-example">A concrete starting Stigma example</h2>

Start with the following four-role model. If fewer slots are unlocked, preserve the recovery your party depends on before adding extra damage.

| Option in the guide | Why it is considered | When to reconsider it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Party damage/protection buff | Adds useful party offense and protection | A Chanter may already cover overlapping effects |
| Resurrection | Restores an ally after a death | Keep it accessible when the group relies on you for recovery |
| Earth's Punishment | Supports the demonstrated damage and debuff sequence | Check the available skill level and interaction in your tooltip |
| Noble Aura | Adds continuing damage | Replace with an extra healing option if the encounter needs it |

Use Absolution or Benevolence in place of Noble Aura when the party needs more healing. Prayer of Amplification is a damage alternative to test. The protection skill's name differs between the captions, so identify it by its party damage/protection effect. [Original Stigma explanation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J3nPw7cuDjY).

aLuckyRO unlocks resurrection early. Equip the available version first; improve its cast later if the party needs it.

<h3>Two choices to make before the dungeon</h3>

**With a Chanter and insufficient healing:** compare the actual buff effects, then replace a redundant protection or Noble Aura slot with Absolution or Benevolence. Test recovery before removing another damage tool.

**Without a Chanter and without a Condemnation reset:** retain needed protection and resurrection, apply the three debuffs manually, and use Condemnation when available. Check its selected option before investing in a reset-based sequence.

<h3>Starter skill and option card</h3>

| Skill / control | Select or prepare | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Debilitating Mark → Chains of Torment → Earth's Punishment | Available debuff skills; select their unlocked options | Opening and refreshing the demonstrated debuff sequence |
| Condemnation | Check whether your selected option supports the critical-hit reset | Use when available; repeat only when the reset actually occurs |
| Radiant Recovery | Select the cleanse option if unlocked | Remove a relevant harmful effect on a separate control |
| Light of Regen | Select the movement-speed option if available | The recovery/utility choice in BoredAF's example |
| Healing Light and Resurrection | Reachable controls outside repeated attacks | Direct recovery and a safe resurrection cast |

**Manual loop:** apply the three debuffs → use available Condemnation and ordinary attacks → refresh expired debuffs. Interrupt for needed healing or cleanse, and cast resurrection in a safe window. If Condemnation does not reset, continue the available attacks and inspect its option. [aLuckyRO's early setup](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UPjsUVC0DtA) demonstrates why a lower-level character needs its own timing.

<h2 id="healing-cleanse-and-resurrection">Healing, cleanse, and resurrection decisions</h2>

Keep a direct heal, a cleanse-capable option, and resurrection on reachable controls outside a repeating damage sequence. Look at the party and encounter before committing a major recovery button. Removing the cause of damage can be more useful than repeatedly healing through it.

BoredAF's later Healing Light skill-level-16 setup is a progression goal in his example, not a Global dungeon entry requirement. The Radiant Recovery cleanse also requires its option; the name alone does not provide it. Follow the [recovery discussion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J3nPw7cuDjY) and the response table below.

<h3>Read the failure before choosing a recovery button</h3>

| What happened | Useful response to check | What to avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An ally takes ordinary damage | An available direct heal and a safe cast position | Continuing damage while recovery is urgently needed |
| A harmful removable effect persists | Whether your selected skill includes the required cleanse | Assuming Radiant Recovery cleanses without the demonstrated option |
| An ally dies during a dangerous mechanic | Resurrection availability and a safe casting window | Starting a cast that makes you the next casualty |
| The party is stable but debuffs have expired | Reapply the intended debuffs, then resume available damage | Assuming the later reset exists because the skill name matches |

**Must I reach the guide's later Healing Light setup before grouping?** It is a progression goal in BoredAF's example, not a verified entry requirement. Assess the current encounter and your available recovery.

**Should I cast resurrection immediately?** Restore the ally when the casting window is safe. Another death caused by ignoring the mechanic can make recovery harder for the whole party.

<h2 id="gear-and-daevanion">Gear and Daevanion priorities without fake thresholds</h2>

BoredAF considers attack, critical effects, double-attack effects, sufficient accuracy, and cooldown reduction in his developed-character example. Use these as attributes to compare, not as universal numeric targets. The right tradeoff depends on your current equipment, content, and whether you are failing to hit, survive, or heal effectively.

Before spending scarce resources, compare a proposed Daevanion route with the skills and passives you use. Avoid investing in a later build's path solely to match its screenshot. Save your current region, level, selected skill options, and equipped Stigmas alongside the plan. The [builds guide](/builds) provides a repeatable setup checklist.
