Chanter is a melee support class that can contribute damage, mantras, and situational recovery. Choose it if you enjoy improving a group's performance while staying active in combat. NC's [Global class introduction](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/about/index) confirms its support identity; the starting examples below come from [aLuckyRO's low-level Chanter guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EsxGbxt0k-4), which simulates early Global progression using an existing live-server character.

**Example scope:** aLuckyRO uses a lower-level TW character to model early Global PvE, reviewed October 4, 2026. The demonstration develops skill-level-8/12 options and shows Dark Crush at skill level 14; level 16 is its later progression example. These are skill levels, not character levels or verified Global unlock requirements. The current Global character-level range and exact point allocation are unverified. Names follow the guide; use only the options visible in your client.

<h2 id="chanter-playstyle">Is Chanter the right class for you?</h2>

Chanter suits players who like switching attention between the enemy and the party. Your personal damage matters, but so does keeping useful buffs available and recognizing when a group needs protection or healing. If you want a character whose main responsibility is the widest healing and cleanse toolkit, compare it with [Cleric](/cleric-build).

[Grobs' class walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RXYBucBwRtk) describes the Chanter's blend of mantras, shielding, healing, and damage. He also distinguishes comfortable dungeon support from the pressure of difficult progression encounters. Before joining a group as its healer, agree that role with the party and prepare an appropriate support configuration.

<h2 id="starting-priorities">A starting priority you can actually follow</h2>

Select unlocked specializations manually, then build the following attack and recovery controls. Raising a skill alone does not select the new option.

<h3>Starter skill and option card</h3>

| Skill | Option shown in the guide | First use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rushing Smash | The on-kill cooldown-reset option when unlocked | Approach and repeated small-enemy pulls |
| Spinning Strike | Cooldown reduction and single-target damage choices | Open an attack window and use it again when ready |
| Impactful Crush | Available attack choice; verify its Dark Crush trigger | Open the next Dark Crush opportunity |
| Dark Crush | Critical-hit option in the lower-level example | Use during the opportunity, then respect its cooldown |
| Recuperation | Percentage-HP recovery option when unlocked | Reachable recovery outside repeated attacks |
| Ordinary attack | Available low-cost attacks between windows | Fill the gap while waiting for the next trigger |

<h3>Stigma card: choose for your party</h3>

| Choice | Starting priority | Swap or check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undefeated Mantra | First mantra investment in the example | Confirm buff coverage with the Cleric |
| Sprint Mantra | Early utility with a smaller investment | Keep the main mantra priority first |
| Two ranged Stigma trigger skills | In the damage example, use their Dark Crush-enabling effects | Match the trigger text; their exact English names are unclear in the captions |
| Protective shield and Healing Touch | Substitute for damage triggers when you must provide support | Equip within unlocked slots and agree the healing role before entering |

**With a Cleric:** start from the mantra-and-trigger model while keeping emergency recovery accessible. **Without a Cleric:** choose the support substitutions before entering; arrange another healer if the available recovery cannot cover the encounter. Unlocking a Stigma and equipping it are separate checks.

<h2 id="party-role-switch">Change your setup when the party changes</h2>

| Situation | Starting adjustment | What to check during the run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo quests | Keep an attack loop and enough recovery to continue safely | Are you losing time to failed pulls or unnecessary downtime? |
| Party with a Cleric | Emphasize damage and useful mantras while retaining emergency utility | Are your buffs active and are you duplicating an effect already covered? |
| Party without a Cleric | Discuss taking healing and protection options before entering | Can you reliably cover the damage and mechanics this party takes? |
| Learning a difficult encounter | Trade a marginal damage option for the support tool the encounter needs | Is a wipe caused by a missed mechanic, insufficient recovery, or avoidable damage? |

These adjustments follow [aLuckyRO's party-dependent examples](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EsxGbxt0k-4). Grobs also reports that some Chanter and Cleric damage buffs overlap. Read the current effects and agree who supplies each one; do not assume that every buff stacks or that a Chanter always replaces a Cleric.

<h2 id="damage-loop">Understand the damage loop before simplifying it</h2>

**Manual starter loop:** Rushing Smash to approach → Spinning Strike → available Dark Crush attacks → wait for Dark Crush's cooldown → Impactful Crush → available Dark Crush attacks. In the next ready window, use an equipped ranged Stigma trigger and its available follow-up. Fill gaps with ordinary attacks and repeat ready triggers instead of pressing all triggers together.

The later Dark Crush skill-level-16 cooldown-removal option changes this pacing in the demonstration. Until that option is present and selected in your client, keep the wait between windows. Test the opening once manually, then repeat while moving.

Keep healing, protection, and other reactive buttons easy to reach. A damage sequence that prevents you from responding to the party is unsuitable for a support role. The [builds guide](/builds) explains how to separate a repeatable attack sequence from decisions that need your attention.

<h3>When the loop fails, check the cause</h3>

| What you observe | First check | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark Crush never becomes available | Selected option and the opening skill's trigger condition | Test one demonstrated trigger manually instead of adding more attacks |
| The first window works but the copied repetition does not | Whether the guide uses a later Dark Crush option | Return to the lower-level timing your character can reproduce |
| Damage continues while an ally needs help | Whether recovery is trapped inside your repeating sequence | Move the reactive skill to a separate, reachable control |

**Can I copy the later rotation if the skill names match?** Matching names alone is insufficient. The selected option, cooldown, and trigger must also match; the guide deliberately demonstrates different behavior at different progression.

**Should I remove all healing when a Cleric joins?** Agree the division of work first. Damage emphasis still leaves a reason to keep emergency utility available, especially while learning an encounter.

<h2 id="progression-and-old-builds">Progression, old builds, and your next change</h2>

Older Chanter notes and newer guides can give different priorities because their skill options and party needs differ. The earlier regional game received a [major class and Stigma overhaul](https://about.ncsoft.com/en/news/article/aion2_update_260325); those changes do not prove that every later feature is available in Global.

Record your region, character level, equipped Stigmas, and the options selected on key skills when saving a setup. Change one problem at a time: improve survival if you cannot stay in combat, add support if the party lacks it, or adjust your attack loop if the expected trigger no longer works. For competitive fights, start with the [PvP mode guide](/pvp) rather than treating this PvE starting example as a complete PvP build.
