<h2 id="what-count-means">Concurrent players, daily players and total accounts</h2>

[Steam's current-player documentation](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/webapi/ISteamUserStats) defines its measure as players active in an app on Steam; it excludes players who are not connected to Steam. Keep that scope beside any number you share.

Concurrency describes simultaneous activity. It cannot tell you how many different people logged in throughout a day, how many accounts exist, or how many players use another client. Do not add a current figure to a peak: those measurements overlap. We have no verified total combining Steam, PURPLE and the regional services.

<h2 id="compare-over-time">Compare player counts without mixing windows</h2>

Save the source, reading time, timezone and metric with each observation. Compare similar weekday and time-of-day slots, and note announcements that may affect access. Use the [maintenance guide](/maintenance) for dated Global announcements.

A single lower reading cannot establish a long-term decline. First check whether one reading is a current figure and the other is a peak, or whether their time windows differ. Several comparable observations make a trend more interpretable; no percentage trend is calculated here because we have not captured a matching series.

<h2 id="choose-a-server">Use population data when choosing a server</h2>

A game-wide Steam count does not show faction balance, your server's group availability or queues. Choose your region and server using the [Global server guide](/server), then assess the activity relevant to your own play hours. The chart is background context, not a per-server census.
