AION 2 has two playable factions: **Elyos and Asmodians**. Choose with your friends before character creation. Faction determines your home server choices and which side you fight in cross-faction modes.

<h2 id="elyos-or-asmodians">Elyos or Asmodians: what differs?</h2>

| Choice | Official world identity | Useful decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elyos | Light-associated faction with Global world locations in Verteron, including Cantas Valley and Dawn Legion Base | Choose it if its world and your group's faction suit you |
| Asmodians | Shadow-associated faction with Global world locations in Altgard, including Safe Haven and Moslan Forest | Choose it if its world and your group's faction suit you |
| Class | Eight classes with different combat roles | Choose your preferred role separately from faction |

Choose the world and faction you prefer, then select your combat role separately from the [class roster](/classes). Preview appearance options in [character creation](/character-creation) before committing.

<h2 id="choose-with-friends">Choose faction and server with friends</h2>

Agree on region, faction and exact server before finalizing the character. A familiar server name alone is insufficient: the same name can appear in different regions. Send a full choice such as Europe, Elyos, Siel rather than only Siel.

Elyos and Asmodians live on separate servers. The opposing server assigned to yours is a war matchup. Use the [server guide](/server) for region and faction lists, then check creation availability in the client.

Instanced content, including dungeons, supports cross-server play. For shared questing with friends, choose the **same region, faction and server**.

<h2 id="cross-faction-opponents">How faction affects your opponents</h2>

An Elyos server is paired with an Asmodian server for cross-faction activities such as the Abyss and Spacetime Rifts. Pairings change periodically to maintain faction balance.

Check the current pairing before coordinating with your group. Use [PvP modes](/pvp) to prepare for combat and [Spacetime Rifts](/spacetime-rift) to plan a visit to enemy territory.

<h2 id="changing-faction">Can a server transfer change your faction?</h2>

**Global server transfers keep your faction.** Transfers begin on **October 14, 2026**; initially, Early Access characters can transfer only to Early Access servers. An Elyos character remains Elyos, and an Asmodian character remains Asmodian.

<h2 id="faction-choice-checklist">A useful final check before creation</h2>

1. Ask your group for its complete region, faction and server choice.
2. Check the client currently allows creation on that server; keep a same-faction backup agreed with everyone.
3. Preview the desired faction's world and appearance options.
4. Select the class whose actual responsibility you enjoy.
5. Read the creation restrictions before confirming your choices.

**Which faction should a beginner choose?** Start with your friends' faction, an available server and the world you prefer.

**Does server pairing give me shared questing with the other faction?** Pairing determines opponents in cross-faction modes. Choose the same faction and home server for ordinary questing together.

**Are faction and class the same choice?** No. Choose Elyos or Asmodians for your faction and select your combat role from the eight classes.
