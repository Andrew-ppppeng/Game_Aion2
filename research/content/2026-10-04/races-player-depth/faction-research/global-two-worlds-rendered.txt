Dawn Legion Base　Deep within Verteron’s Cantas Valley lies a hidden stronghold of the Dawn Legion Base—a secret base that serves as their command center.

Cantas Valley　Once a vital Odylium supply hub for the Daeva,this land was abandoned after its resources were depleted.Now, the Dawn Legion gathers what little remains.

Ellun River Marsh　A high ground along the lower Ellun River,once home to Verteron’s Barrier Tower, destroyed by the Ishtar Legion.Now occupied by the Illusion Cavaliers, who seek to rebuild it.

Aullaeu Village　An Aullaeu settlement on the edge of Tolbas Forest,home to servants of Balaurand a key site tied to the forest’s disturbances.

Altamia Canyon　A canyon in eastern Verteron, once home to the Zumion Temple.Submerged after the Battle of Meslamtaeda, it has since reemerged—now under the watch of the Notos Legion.

Safe Haven　A human settlement in northern Altgard, once a haven for criminals and fugitives. After the fall of the Dredgion, it became an unlikely refuge, spared from the Balaur attacks

Dredgion Crash Site　This canyon marks the crash site of Dredgion from the Fafnir Legion.The impact scorched the land, and the leaking Drakana left the soil corrupted.Now, the area stands as a hazardous zone, cut off from the outside world.

Sanctum Outpost　Once a sacred Archon sanctuary centered on Zikel's Sanctum, this land fell in battle against Fafnir and is now used as a Balaur stronghold. Now, Draconute Warriors capture Daevas to turn them into Fafnites.

Moslan Forest　A forest between northern Altgard and the Basfelt Ruins,once a sanctuary for the Elim and the Nornir. Burned by the High Priest Hystan, it is now left only with the wounded Elim.

Nornir Assembly　A Nornir settlement built by refugees from the Moslan Forest, shielded by ancient magic and led by Urd of the Three Sisters of Fate.

The Fallen Aion Tower　At the end of the Elyos–Asmodian War, the Aion Tower fell.No barrier was ever raised, and the war between Elyos and Asmodians consumed even the light of the world.Thus, that day would forever be remembered as the “the Last War.”

A Divided World　When the Tower fell and the barrier vanished,the Balaur surged into Elysea.In their final act, the Empyrean Lords gathered what strength remained to raise a Bulwark of light—a radiant veil that split the world and sealed Elysea in silent isolation.

Siel’s Absence, The Line Drawn in Blood　Built upon countless sacrifices, the Bulwark was completed at great cost.Siel vanished, and the remaining Lords gave all they had.Some were lost, while others were stripped of their light.

The Shadow of the Dragon Lord　As the barrier vanished, Atreia was cast into war.Dragon Lords, Ishtar and Fafnir advanced relentlessly, consuming Poeta in fire and darkness.In their wake, countless lives fell.

A Tarnished Blessing—The Fall of the Daeva　Beyond the Bulwark, the Daeva’s Awakening was no longer a blessing.They were hunted, turned into fuel of Surana, and reborn as twisted aberrations that preyed upon their own kind.

The Fallen Aion Tower　At the end of the Elyos–Asmodian War, the Aion Tower fell.No barrier was ever raised, and the war between Elyos and Asmodians consumed even the light of the world.Thus, that day would forever be remembered as the “the Last War.”

A Fractured World　The day the barrier fell, Asmodae was left defenseless. The Balaur poured in like a tide, swallowing everything in darkness. In response, the Empyrean Lords gathered their final strength to raise a Bulwark of light, sealing the realm in shadow.

Israphel’s Absence, the Bulwark Built in Blood　Israphel, guardian of the Tower, is no more.The five remaining Empyrean Lords unleashed the last of their power, staking everything they had.Forged from their blood and souls, the Bulwark became the Asmodae’s final shield.

The Age of Two Dragon Lords　Beyond the Bulwark, the world was a field ripe for conquest.The two Dragon Lords, Ishtar and Fafnir, trampled Ishalgen and devoured the continent.Though the Bulwark was completed and the advance halted, the sacrifices of the nameless continued unabated. Beyond the Bulwark, the land fell under the Dragon Lords’ rule

Fafnite, the shattered soul　The Daeva, meant to walk in the light, were swallowed by darkness.Fafnir tore their souls apart, binding their light within gems.Those who once carried the breath of the gods became agents of destruction.
