Comprehensive class overhaul enhances combat balance and introduces new Stigma skills for every class
New PvP content Chaotic Abyss features faction-based battles between West and East Forces
New Skins, Pets, and Wings revealed, along with the introduction of Kina (Bound) and separation of PvP/PvE damage coefficients
NCSOFT today announced major class overhaul update for its MMORPG,
AION 2
, alongside the introduction of a new gameplay feature, Chaotic Abyss.
Available starting today, the update introduces a wide range of improvements and new content, including class balance adjustments, new Stigma skills, Chaotic Abyss, cosmetic additions, and Kina (Bound).
A comprehensive class rebalance has been implemented to improve combat balance across all classes. As part of this update, Immune and Immortal effects can no longer be applied consecutively within a 30-second window. Additionally, new defensive mechanics—Perfect Shield Block and Perfect Parry—have been introduced to enhance survivability.
New Stigma skills have been added to highlight each class’s unique identity. These include Fracturing Rush (Gladiator), Battlefield Banner (Templar), Barrier Spell (Chanter), Reincarnating Touch (Cleric), Command: Proxy (Elementalist), Hibernation (Sorcerer), Shadowstep (Assassin), and Supporting Fire (Ranger). The maximum number of Stigma slots has also been expanded to five, improving overall usability.
The update also introduces Chaotic Abyss, a newly designed PvP system that reinterprets traditional RvR gameplay. Instead of race-based factions, players are divided into West and East forces at the server level, engaging in large-scale battles regardless of race alignment. Meanwhile, Spacetime Rift will continue to operate under the current race-based matchmaking system.
NCSOFT also unveiled new cosmetic additions, including three Skins—Black Cat, Sporty Chic Fit, and Warm-Hearted Maid. Players can also obtain the new Creepy Night Cat Wings, along with two new Pets—Babymeow and Aristomeow.
Reflecting player feedback, Kina (Bound) has been added to the game. Kina (Bound) can be earned through quests and Duty rewards, NPC shops, and content such as Sealed Dungeons and Strongholds. Regular Kina remains obtainable through player trading, monster hunting, and Odyle Energy Cubes. In addition, PvP and PvE damage calculations have been separated, and balance adjustments have been made to reduce damage gaps between certain skills, supporting a more diverse combat experience.
