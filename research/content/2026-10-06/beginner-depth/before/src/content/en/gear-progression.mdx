<h2 id="three-progression-numbers">Level, gear score and Combat Power</h2>

Character level records experience progression. Gear score or item level describes equipment progression and can be an entry requirement. Combat Power includes wider character strength, including skills and other growth systems. Read the exact number used by the activity before upgrading. Raising Combat Power does not complete a missing campaign quest, and reaching level 45 does not automatically satisfy an equipment gate.

<h2 id="fresh-level-45">Your first steps at 45</h2>

Finish the remaining main story and review useful quest rewards. Clear accessible sealed dungeons and strongholds you skipped, and collect nearby feathers while following those objectives. Review the skill points and Daevanion resources obtained from exploration before spending more equipment materials. Open your next activity’s panel, select its difficulty and check unlock, equipment and reward requirements separately. Choose an activity you can complete reliably.

<h2 id="growth-and-enhancement">Preview an equipment upgrade</h2>

Inspect each item’s upgrade panel rather than assuming all equipment uses one rule. Compare the current item with the preview: stat changes, required materials, currency and any failure condition shown. Upgrade one piece for a specific purpose, such as a weapon improvement or a missing entry requirement. Keep enough materials for that operation; do not spread your supply across several temporary pieces without checking how much progress each buys.

<h2 id="belt-and-amulet">Belt and amulet upgrades</h2>

Belt and amulet progression can involve enhancement followed by a Substance Morph recipe. Open that recipe and match the exact input item, its required enhancement and all materials. Remove the item from equipped presets when the recipe requires an unequipped input. Check the output before accepting: a matching appearance or name alone is not enough. Keep the input until the recipe is ready, rather than dismantling it during inventory cleanup.

<h2 id="sockets-binding-and-transfer">Sockets, binding and transfer</h2>

Check the available Manastone and Theostone slots on your actual weapon or armor before buying stones. Read the binding or reroll confirmation separately from enhancement: cost and affected stats are different decisions. For transfer, select the actual source and destination and inspect exactly which progress is retained. Never dispose of the old item before confirming the result. An item’s trade, storage and extraction permissions are separate flags; read them before making an irreversible inventory choice.

<h2 id="next-upgrade">Choose the next useful upgrade</h2>

If entry is locked, resolve the listed requirement. If a boss kills you, review attacks, positioning and recovery before adding gear score. If damage or MP is the problem, inspect skills, specializations and Daevanion as well as equipment. Save crafting materials for a named recipe and compare its cost with the reward route you can already complete. After upgrading, reopen the activity panel and retest the same encounter to confirm the blocker is resolved.
