<h2 id="start-global">Start with the right account and server</h2>

AION 2 is free-to-play on Steam. Public access opened on October 5, 2026 at 13:00 UTC. Install the main game, App 3393110, and sign in with your playing account.

For a first session, use this order:

1. Follow the [official-client download guide](/download) and finish updates.
2. Agree on your region, faction and exact [server](/server) with friends.
3. Choose a [class](/classes) for the role you enjoy.
4. Finish [character creation](/character-creation), then check movement, camera and combat bindings.
5. Start the main story and revisit new skill options as the game introduces them.

**Before the first quest:** check potion auto-use, loot selection and HUD readability. Look for **Combat → Auto Use**, **Combat → Looting** and **HUD Edit**, or their equivalents in your client language. Set potion thresholds for your available supply, and review automatic loot or disposal rules before enabling them.

Before questing, use the [settings guide](/settings) to adjust targeting, bindings, HUD and graphics.

<h2 id="how-to-play-by-region">Choose your server region</h2>

Choose **NA West, NA East, Europe, South America or Asia** in the client. Match the region, faction and exact server with friends. Steam and the official PURPLE service share the same game servers.

<h2 id="main-story-first">Use the main story as your progression spine</h2>

Follow the main story and read the rewards and unlock requirements in your quest panel. Character level alone does not replace campaign progress. When a new activity opens, finish its introduction before starting repeated runs.

When the story stops, inspect the actual requirement before grinding:

| Blocker | Check | Useful next step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quest is not available | Previous quest completion and required character level | Reopen the journal and finish the missing step |
| Ascension/progression requirement | Its displayed gauge or objective | Complete nearby eligible regional content shown by your client |
| Boss kills you | Avoidable attacks, weapon, skills and consumables | Learn the mechanic, then make a targeted improvement |
| Dungeon entry is locked | Its current level/item requirement and unlock quest | Complete that requirement rather than raising an unrelated number |

Use the [leveling guide](/leveling) for faction-specific progression and the next activities to prioritize.

**The destination is visible, but you cannot continue:** open the objective and identify its missing requirement. For an Ascension gate, complete eligible regional objectives. For an equipment gate, compare your gear with the entry requirement. Return to the campaign objective once that requirement is met.

<h2 id="skills-and-gear">Improve skills and gear with a clear purpose</h2>

Character level, item level or gear score, and Combat Power measure different parts of progression. Check which requirement an activity uses before spending materials. Improve the specific blocker first, then refine your skills, equipment and other growth systems.

- Equip useful quest rewards as you progress; compare their actual stats instead of chasing rarity alone.
- Read your most-used damage, defensive and support skills when new options unlock.
- If your client offers an in-built skill-combination feature, configure it for your available skills and bindings. Do not copy a different class's sequence blindly.
- Spend upgrade materials to solve a clear weakness or entry requirement; expect to replace early equipment.
- Read the current enhancement confirmation, especially when an item has a distinct failure rule.

Use [builds and skills](/builds) to choose skill options, and the [macro guide](/macro-guide) to simplify a short repeatable attack sequence.

<h2 id="keep-materials">Keep materials until you know their use</h2>

Keep unfamiliar crafting and progression materials until you know their use. Read the item's tooltip and relevant recipe before selling, extracting or deleting it.

Move materials you do not need immediately into storage. Review quick-storage and automatic-extraction selection rules, and protect equipment needed by a quest or supply request.

For gathering, choose a material required by a recipe or upgrade you plan to complete. Use the [gathering guide](/gathering) to build a compact route.

<h2 id="exploration-checklist">Make exploration part of ordinary travel</h2>

| When you encounter it | What to inspect |
| --- | --- |
| New travel point | Whether it is activated and usable for return travel |
| Nearby feather or exploration collectible | Current collection progress and the associated reward screen |
| Sealed dungeon | Required level, objective and one-time progression rewards |
| Stronghold | Completion state and the equipment materials shown |
| Pet or other persistent growth system | Available unlocks while doing your regular quests |

Take nearby collectibles while leveling, then schedule a dedicated cleanup session. Use the [map guide](/map) to enable relevant layers and avoid confusing a cave point with one on the surface. Opposite-faction exploration can expose you to [PvP](/pvp); it is not an essential first-session errand.

<h2 id="next-session">Plan the next session and the move to level 45</h2>

<GuideVisual id="workflow" />

The launch level cap is **45**. Finish remaining story objectives and inspect the activities unlocked at that milestone before building your repeatable routine.

Start each session with one progression goal: a story block, a skill improvement, a collection area or your next dungeon. Check limited reward counters and reset countdowns before spending an entry or claiming a reward.

Claim [official codes](/code) and [Twitch Drops](/twitch-drops) through the appropriate account when available. End by checking bag space, your next quest and the requirement for your next activity. That gives you an actionable starting point without trying to finish every system at once.
