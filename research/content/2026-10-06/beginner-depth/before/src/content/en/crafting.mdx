<h2 id="start-with-a-recipe">Choose the result before the profession</h2>

Open Crafting and find the item you intend to make. Read the profession, required crafting level, output and material list before spending progression resources. Start with a recipe that contributes to an equipment upgrade or a consumable you actually use. Do not choose a profession solely because someone calls it the best: its useful recipes, ingredient demand and your available materials determine whether it serves your character.

<h2 id="different-material-systems">Gathering, extraction and morphing</h2>

Essence Extraction is gathering from resource nodes. Item Extraction dismantles equipment; it is not the same proficiency bar. Substance Morph converts listed inputs through a recipe, including some equipment progression recipes. Keep the source item until you have checked its uses and the recipe output. A gathering promotion quest item is not interchangeable with a similarly named ordinary crafting material. Match the full name, quality and binding state.

<h2 id="prepare-a-batch">Prepare one batch of materials</h2>

Record the recipe’s required quantity of each material and the quantity already available. Subtract your usable stock before planning a gathering route or purchase. Check whether the recipe accepts the item you own, including Bound variants. Read crafting quantity carefully: changing the batch size can multiply both ingredients and currency. Reserve equipment or materials used by another quest before confirming consumption.

<h2 id="gather-or-buy">Gather or buy the missing inputs</h2>

For a gatherable input, inspect the node’s requirement and your Essence Extraction stage. A short nearby route is useful only if it yields the recipe’s actual material. For a purchase, compare the total quantity price rather than one listing’s unit price. Market access has membership conditions, so include access cost and availability in that comparison. The material budget tool can record your own quantities and prices without assuming a fixed market value.

<h2 id="first-craft">Complete and inspect the first craft</h2>

Select the intended recipe and start with one batch. Recheck output, ingredients, currency and the displayed conditions before confirming. Afterwards, inspect the inventory result and the profession progress separately. If the item cannot be used, compare class, level and binding conditions before crafting another copy. Do not assume an exceptional outcome or a guaranteed success rate when the recipe does not show one. Keep the actual result as your reference for the next batch.

<h2 id="crafting-blockers">Fix a blocked recipe</h2>

If the recipe is locked, read its profession or unlock requirement. If materials appear missing despite your inventory, compare the exact name and eligible variant, then check the selected batch quantity. For an equipment input, check whether it remains equipped in a preset. If a limit is exhausted, read that recipe’s refresh information rather than treating it as a gathering problem. Stop when the next batch does not advance your chosen item or profession goal; save materials for the next useful recipe.
