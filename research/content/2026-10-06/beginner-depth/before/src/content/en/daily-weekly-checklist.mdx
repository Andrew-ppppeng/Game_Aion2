<h2 id="different-counters">Use the right counter</h2>

A daily and weekly checklist organizes your available activities. A campaign completion reward, a regenerating energy bar and a reward counter with a reset are different systems. Open the activity and read entry conditions, reward conditions and the refresh indicator separately. Do not treat the words “Daily Dungeon” as proof that its reward allowance refills every day.

<h2 id="duty-missions">Duty missions after level 45</h2>

Open Journal → Duty after reaching 45. Inspect the available mission, its destination, required objective and reward choices before accepting. Group nearby objectives to reduce travel and use a reward needed for your current progression. The October 5 launch update included a fix for Duty access after reaching 45; the old workaround to avoid opening the tab before leveling is obsolete. If the panel remains unavailable, record the client error and contact support.

<h2 id="energy-and-rewards">Entry, energy and reward claims</h2>

For Expeditions and other limited activities, choose difficulty before checking requirements. Read the energy or ticket charge and the reward chest separately: completing a run and claiming its reward can be different steps. Before spending Odyle Energy, check the reward you want and whether your remaining supply supports another useful claim. A locked reward counter is not automatically an equipment problem. Inspect the displayed reason before repeating the run.

<h2 id="before-a-reset">Review before the displayed reset</h2>

At your weekly review, inspect each unlocked activity’s remaining rewards and its actual next reset. Finish useful available rewards before their displayed deadline, rather than following a universal weekday. Check whether the allowance belongs to the current character, server or account before switching characters. Shop limits and recipe limits must be read on the actual offer; a calendar week alone does not establish their refresh rule.

<h2 id="one-time-and-events">One-time rewards and events</h2>

Keep sealed dungeons, stronghold completion and campaign rewards on a separate exploration list. Their first-clear benefits should not be copied into a repeating routine. Check mail for launch gifts and claim a coupon through its own menu; Twitch Drops require the linked playing account and Twitch inventory claim. Each promotion has its own deadline. A daily task reset does not extend an event or an expiring mail reward.

<h2 id="short-session">A useful short session</h2>

Start by inspecting Duty, then choose one activity that supplies the resource for your next upgrade. Read its allowance before entry, complete the objective and confirm the reward was claimed. If the next step needs a quest unlock or a one-time exploration reward, do that instead of an unrelated repeated run. Before logging out, note remaining counters, the next displayed deadline and the exact material still missing. Read this checklist alongside your equipment goal, not as a requirement to complete everything.
