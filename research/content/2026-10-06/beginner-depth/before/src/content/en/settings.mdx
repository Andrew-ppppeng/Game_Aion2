<h2 id="control-mode">Choose a control mode</h2>

Open Settings → Combat and review the available movement and camera controls. Try camera movement, selecting an enemy and turning away from it before combat. Keep the mode in which you can move and use your emergency buttons without losing sight of the fight. Check the current bindings rather than assuming a key from another setup is still assigned.

<h2 id="targeting-and-pursuit">Target scanning and pursuit</h2>

Under Combat → Target Scanning, inspect the preferred search direction. Front of camera helps select enemies you are looking toward. Under Combat → Controls, inspect pursuit with skill activation. Melee pursuit can help close distance; a ranged character may prefer it off to avoid walking into the pack. Test selecting a nearer enemy with another enemy behind you, then cast while outside range and observe whether your character moves.

<h2 id="potions-and-loot">Potion auto-use and loot</h2>

Combat → Auto Use controls automatic HP consumables. Choose a threshold that fits the damage you take and your potion supply; leave manual access available. A high threshold can consume potions during minor damage. In Combat → Looting, review selections before enabling automatic rules. Protect equipment needed by quests or recipes and read extraction selections before confirming disposal.

<h2 id="keybinds-and-hud">Key bindings and a readable HUD</h2>

Place movement, dodge or block, interrupt, healing and other encounter reactions on separate reachable controls. Use the built-in macro for a tested repeatable attack stack, not as your only access to a recovery skill. Open HUD Edit and keep HP, MP, target information and party frames readable. In Info Display → Common, adjust nameplates. Try the layout in combat: your eyes should find health and the next mechanic without searching across overlapping panels.

<h2 id="graphics-and-crowds">Graphics and crowded fights</h2>

For large groups, inspect identical player appearance and other-player effect visibility. Limit effects to self or party when visual clutter hides mechanics. Compare lower shadows and reflections in the same scene. Upscaling options depend on your GPU and the options offered by the client; compare image clarity as well as frame rate. No single preset guarantees an FPS increase on every PC. Keep enemy warnings and useful party effects visible.

<h2 id="test-your-settings">Test before keeping the changes</h2>

Record your starting settings and change one group at a time. Repeat a short encounter using the same camera angle and route. Check unintended movement, target selection, potion consumption, readable warnings and frame stability. If a change makes a reaction harder, restore that setting first. Repeat the test after changing control mode or moving an emergency skill. A smooth empty field does not tell you how a crowded boss fight will behave.
