<h2 id="creation-decisions">Choose your region, faction and class first</h2>

Agree on a **region, faction and server** with your group before spending time on appearance. AION 2 has Elyos, Asmodians and eight classes. Use [classes](/classes) to choose a combat role and [servers](/server) to choose where to play.

| Decision | Check before confirming |
| --- | --- |
| Region | The region your group will actually play in, rather than the language of a tutorial |
| Faction | Elyos or Asmodians; coordinate the choice with friends |
| Server | Exact server name and region, and whether it currently accepts creation |
| Class | Preferred combat role, range and support responsibilities |
| Appearance | A starting template you can refine and inspect from several angles |

If your chosen server has queues or creation restrictions, agree on a recommended backup server with your group. Server transfers start on **October 14, 2026**, stay within the same faction and initially restrict Early Access characters to Early Access servers.

<h2 id="appearance-workflow">Build the appearance from broad shapes to details</h2>

<GuideVisual id="workflow" />

AION 2 offers over 200 customization options. Establish the overall silhouette first, then refine the face, hair and styling.

1. Select the starting template and compare the available base appearances. Begin near your target instead of fighting every slider.
2. Set broad face and body proportions while checking both front and side views.
3. Refine eyes, brows, nose and mouth. Change one feature at a time so you can see which adjustment caused a result.
4. Choose hair shape and colors, then inspect how it frames the face.
5. Add makeup, tattoos, scars or other details after the main proportions work.
6. Preview the full character under different lighting and clothing before confirming.

**Adjust one feature at a time:** keep the starting template, hairstyle and lighting fixed. Inspect the nose and chin from the front and profile, then change only one relevant slider. If a numerical value is shown, note it before adjusting. Rotate again before moving a second slider; restore the noted value if the profile became worse. This makes the effect of one edit visible instead of masking it with several simultaneous changes.

<h2 id="face-hair-details">Check the face, hair and detail panels</h2>

| Area | Useful checks | Common mistake |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eyes and brows | Shape, spacing, iris/color choices and any asymmetry controls | Judging only from a close frontal view |
| Nose and mouth | Profile, projection and balance with the jaw | Making a small frontal adjustment that changes the side silhouette |
| Hair | Primary/secondary colors, shine and the face outline | Matching a screenshot color under only one lighting preset |
| Makeup and markings | Placement and visibility under the chosen hair | Adding strong details before the face is settled |
| Body | Overall template, proportions and layered adjustment controls | Assessing the face without checking the full-body result |

<h2 id="preview-checklist">Preview before you finalize</h2>

- Rotate to front, profile and three-quarter views. Check the nose, chin, ears and hair from each.
- Compare close-up and full-body views. A pleasing portrait may have different proportions at gameplay distance.
- Try more than one lighting setup and an outfit preview where the editor offers them.
- Recheck hair and makeup after changing the face: they may now hide or emphasize a different feature.
- Verify the character's class, faction and server selection before the final creation action.
- Follow the name format and validation shown on the creation screen.

If a server prevents creation, check the server-selection screen and [current notices](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/list) for its creation status.

**When creation fails:** read the error first. For a name error, follow the permitted format shown on that screen. For a server restriction, check the selected server's creation status. For an appearance problem, return to the preview and compare the same camera angle and lighting before changing settings.

<h2 id="shared-presets">Use shared styles and plan later appearance changes</h2>

Browse the [official Style Shop](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/styleshop/popular) for shared appearances and inspiration. Select your region and compare several previews. The [presets guide](/presets) explains downloading a style to an existing character and opening it in **Manage Appearance**.

Before applying an appearance change to an existing character, check the Quna cost or voucher eligibility on the confirmation screen. After creating your first character, check your controls and continue with the [beginner guide](/guide).

Before saving a character, use the [faction guide](/races) to check Elyos, Asmodian and grouping restrictions. A cosmetic preset does not change the faction and server choices that determine where you start.
