AION 2 … Skills … Gladiator Rage Burst Rage Burst Gladiator stigma skill · unlocks at level 22 · max level 25 Gladiator Stigma skill Rage Burst is a Gladiator stigma skill in AION 2 that unlocks at level 22 and can be raised to skill level 25. Its cooldown at skill level 1 is 45s. Deals 1,327 (+218% Attack) damage to a target within 4m. Inflicts Wounded for 10s on hit, reducing the enemy's Attack by 10%, and increases the caster's PvE Damage Boost by 10% and PvP Damage Boost by 5% for 10s. Triggers [Overhead Slam] on the target for 10s on hit.
Wounded does not stack with the Shrink effect from the Templar skill [Taunt].
