AION 2 … Skills … Templar Shield Rush Shield Rush Templar active skill · unlocks at level 10 · max level 40 Templar Active skill Shield Rush is a Templar active skill in AION 2 that unlocks at level 10 and can be raised to skill level 40. Its cooldown at skill level 1 is 20s. Rushes to a target within 10m and deals 258 (+105% Attack) damage after using [Dodge] or while flying. Has a 30% chance to inflict Stun for 3s. Triggers [Judgment] for 2s.
100% chance to inflict Stun on NPC targets.
