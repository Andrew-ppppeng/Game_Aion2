AION 2 … Skills … Sorcerer Arctic Armor Arctic Armor Sorcerer stigma skill · unlocks at level 22 · max level 25 Sorcerer Stigma skill Arctic Armor is a Sorcerer stigma skill in AION 2 that unlocks at level 22 and can be raised to skill level 25. Its cooldown at skill level 1 is 90s. Increases the caster's PvE Damage Tolerance by 20%, PvP Damage Tolerance by 10%, and grants a 5% chance to inflict Frost for 3s on enemies that attack the caster from within 7m for 10s. Grants Magic Displacement while MP is 50% or more for the duration.
Magic Displacement: Damage taken by the caster is divided, directing 50% to HP and 10% to MP.
