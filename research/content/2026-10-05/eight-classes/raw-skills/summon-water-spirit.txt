AION 2 … Skills … Spiritmaster Summon: Water Spirit Summon: Water Spirit Spiritmaster active skill · unlocks at level 3 · max level 40 Spiritmaster Active skill Summon: Water Spirit is a Spiritmaster active skill in AION 2 that unlocks at level 3 and can be raised to skill level 40. Its cooldown at skill level 1 is 15s. Summons a Water Spirit.
The Water Spirit uses a skill that deals 143 (+145% Attack) Water damage to a target within 20m.
The Water Spirit fights alongside the caster using basic attacks, with a 15% chance to use its skill and restoring 20 of the Spiritmaster's MP each time an attack lands. It is dismissed upon selecting the summon icon.
Each skill level increases the Water Spirit's Attack and Critical Hit by 2%.
