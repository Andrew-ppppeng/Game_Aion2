AION 2 … Skills … Spiritmaster Summon: Ancient Spirit Summon: Ancient Spirit Spiritmaster stigma skill · unlocks at level 22 · max level 25 Spiritmaster Stigma skill Summon: Ancient Spirit is a Spiritmaster stigma skill in AION 2 that unlocks at level 22 and can be raised to skill level 25. Its cooldown at skill level 1 is 90s. Summons an Ancient Spirit for 30s and grants Four Elements status each time the Ancient Spirit uses its skill.
The Ancient Spirit uses a skill on a target within 20m, dealing 1,082 (+189% Attack) damage to up to 4 enemies within 4m.
The Ancient Spirit fights alongside the caster using basic attacks and reuses its skill after 7 basic attacks. It is dismissed upon selecting the summon icon.
Each skill level increases the Ancient Spirit's Attack, Defense, Accuracy, and Critical Hit by 2%.
