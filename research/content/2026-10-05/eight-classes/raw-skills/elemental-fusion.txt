The Spiritmaster receives an element each time a Spirit uses its skill. Grants Four Elements status and the ability to use the [Elemental Fusion] skill upon reaching 4 stacks. [Elemental Fusion] can only be used when granted Four Elements, and using [Elemental Fusion] will immediately remove Four Elements. Cannot receive additional elements while in Four Elements status.
