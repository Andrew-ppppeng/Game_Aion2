AION 2 … Skills … Spiritmaster Command: Proxy Command: Proxy Spiritmaster stigma skill · unlocks at level 22 · max level 25 Spiritmaster Stigma skill Command: Proxy is a Spiritmaster stigma skill in AION 2 that unlocks at level 22 and can be raised to skill level 25. Its cooldown at skill level 1 is 60s. Increases the Spirit's PvE Damage Tolerance by 20% and PvP Damage Tolerance by 10% for 4s, and redirects damage taken each time the Spiritmaster is attacked, sharing 50% to the Spiritmaster and 50% to the Spirit.
This effect is applied to the summoned Spirit with the highest HP. If HP is equal, it will be applied to the closer target. Damage is only shared if the Spirit is within 25m.
