The Aura is stationary and periodically deals 394 (+431.1% Attack) Earth damage to a selected target within 20m. What is the cooldown of Divine Aura? 30 seconds at level 1 and 30 seconds at level 40. When does the Cleric learn Divine Aura? At character level 3. It can then be raised to level 40. What are the specializations of Divine Aura? Divine Aura has 5 specializations. Each is unlocked with its own stigma item once the skill reaches the required level; see the list above for what each one changes. How do I raise Divine Aura above its max level? Take its skill-level nodes on the Daevanion boards (Nezekan, Zikel, Vaizel, and Triniel). There are 4 such nodes for Divine Aura.
