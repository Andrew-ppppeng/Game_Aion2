AION 2 … Skills … Spiritmaster Summon: Earth Spirit Summon: Earth Spirit Spiritmaster active skill · unlocks at level 7 · max level 40 Spiritmaster Active skill Summon: Earth Spirit is a Spiritmaster active skill in AION 2 that unlocks at level 7 and can be raised to skill level 40. Its cooldown at skill level 1 is 15s. Summons an Earth Spirit.
The Earth Spirit uses a skill that deals 247 (+135% Attack) Earth damage to a target within 20m and increases Enmity by 3,750. Has a 30% chance to inflict Taunt for 2s if the target is a player.
The Earth Spirit fights alongside the caster using basic attacks, with a 10% chance to use its skill and increasing Enmity each time an attack lands. It is dismissed upon selecting the summon icon.
Each skill level increases the Earth Spirit's Defense and HP by 2%.
