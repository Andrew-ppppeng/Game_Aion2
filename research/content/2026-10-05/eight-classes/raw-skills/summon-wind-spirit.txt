AION 2 … Skills … Spiritmaster Summon: Wind Spirit Summon: Wind Spirit Spiritmaster active skill · unlocks at level 10 · max level 40 Spiritmaster Active skill Summon: Wind Spirit is a Spiritmaster active skill in AION 2 that unlocks at level 10 and can be raised to skill level 40. Its cooldown at skill level 1 is 15s. Summons a Wind Spirit.
The Wind Spirit selects a target within 20m and uses a skill that deals 273 (+111% Attack) Wind damage to up to 4 enemies within 5m of the target and restores 134 (+100% Attack)-147 HP to the Spiritmaster.
The Wind Spirit fights alongside the caster using basic attacks, with a 10% chance to use its skill and restores 1.5% of the Spiritmaster's HP each time an attack lands. It is dismissed upon selecting the summon icon.
Each skill level increases the Wind Spirit's Accuracy and Critical Hit by 2%.
