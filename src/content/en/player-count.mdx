<h2 id="what-count-means">Concurrent players, daily players and total accounts</h2>

Steam concurrency counts players active in the game at the same time. It covers Steam activity rather than every Global client.

A current count, a 24-hour peak and an all-time peak measure different things. Daily players count distinct users over a day; total accounts count registrations. Keep these metrics separate and do not add current concurrency to a peak.
