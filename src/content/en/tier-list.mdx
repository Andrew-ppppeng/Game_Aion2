<h2 id="what-each-list-measures">What each list actually measures</h2>

CrusherXGaming mixes early solo comfort, party contribution, and Abyss utility in a Global launch recommendation. His Ranger discussion refers to the level-37 launch test, not a controlled full-level comparison. The video also includes claims about launch rules; those claims do not become verified rules simply because they appear next to the rankings.

Wiggles describes taking the first 100 entries from four PvE leaderboard categories in each of KR and TW. That produces 800 leaderboard entries. The same character can appear in more than one category, so the sample is not proven to contain 800 distinct players. He combines representation with his judgment of survivability, damage uptime, and group usefulness. It is neither a random sample of all players nor a direct measurement of Global class damage. See the [original explanation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mCxLbeDusUg).

Both videos were reviewed for this page on October 2, 2026. Their exact client builds and independently reproducible testing conditions are not established here. Keep that limitation attached to the ratings when sharing them.

<h2 id="spiritmaster-and-ranger">Why Spiritmaster and Ranger disagree so much</h2>

CrusherXGaming values Spiritmaster's summons for safe early solo play and its control tools for Abyss encounters. Wiggles focuses on competitive PvE results and reports low representation in his regional leaderboard sample. Those observations can coexist: comfortable questing and strong crowd control do not automatically produce the fastest boss clear.

Ranger shows the other large split, B versus S. A limited-level test and a later regional leaderboard sample include different progression, gear, and encounter conditions. Treat the difference as a reason to inspect the gameplay and patch context, not evidence that one author has discovered a universal truth. The two sources do not support averaging their tiers into a new numeric score.

<h3>Worked choice: Spiritmaster for a first solo character</h3>

Suppose your plan is questing and exploration with occasional dungeons. CrusherXGaming's S+ is relevant to his stated view of summon-assisted solo comfort, while Wiggles' D addresses his regional competitive PvE assessment. Keep Spiritmaster on your shortlist if you enjoy managing spirits. Before committing, watch it through a moving encounter and ask whether you can coordinate your attacks and summons while handling mechanics. Neither rating answers that personal control question.

Now change the goal to an organized group pursuing faster PvE clears. Inspect a full encounter from the progression you will actually reach, with party composition and equipment visible. Wiggles' regional sample is a reason to investigate that context; it is not evidence that a fresh Global Spiritmaster cannot clear the dungeon.

<h3>Worked choice: Ranger B versus S</h3>

Do not reroll solely because the launch-opinion column says B. Compare the limited-level context with the later regional context, then identify the problem in your own run: loss of attacks while moving, unavailable options, or failed mechanics. If the problem is maintaining range, test a positioning change before buying into a different class.

**If both authors give A, are those classes equivalent?** No. A letter does not measure identical damage, responsibility, or ease of play. Templar and Cleric still solve different party problems.

**Which column should I use for PvP?** Wiggles' PvE column is not a PvP rating. Use mode-specific gameplay and your team's needs; the mixed launch column also does not provide a controlled PvP result.

<h2 id="test-before-rerolling">A practical check before you reroll</h2>

First, try the class with the skill options actually unlocked on your character. Then run the same encounter more than once and note why an attempt failed: mechanics, uptime, missing healing, or an incomplete setup. Compare under similar level and equipment conditions, and record whether party buffs were present. A short solo dummy attempt should not be treated as the whole party's contribution.

Check the guide's region and date again after a balance update. NC's [March 25 regional overhaul](https://about.ncsoft.com/en/news/article/aion2_update_260325) changed classes, Stigma availability, and PvE/PvP calculations, illustrating why an older build or test can become misleading. Keep your main if its role and controls fit you; change only after identifying a problem that a different playstyle would actually solve.
