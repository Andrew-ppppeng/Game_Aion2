Choose your **region, faction and server** with friends before creating a character. Each Global server hosts one faction; opposing-faction servers are paired for cross-faction play.

<h2 id="choose-with-friends">Choose a server with your friends</h2>

1. Agree on the region first: NA West, NA East, Europe, South America, or Asia.
2. Agree on Elyos or Asmodian. Your faction determines the server list you see.
3. Check whether everyone is joining Advanced Access or public launch.
4. Choose a preferred server and a backup in that same region and faction.
5. Check the client's current creation availability before committing to the plan.

If your preferred server has a queue, consider a recommended server in the same region and faction. Check creation availability in the client before your group commits.

Choose a region that fits your group's location and usual play hours, then check your connection.

<h2 id="cross-server-play">What server pairing and cross-server play mean</h2>

Pairing determines the opposing faction you meet in Spacetime Rifts and the Abyss. Pairings are adjusted periodically.

Instanced content, including dungeons, supports cross-server play. For open-world questing together, choose the same region, faction and server.

Use the [PvP guide](/pvp) for the distinction between modes, and the [rift guide](/spacetime-rift) for entering the opposing faction's territory.

<h2 id="transfer-rules">Transfers and public launch</h2>

| Question | Transfer rule |
| --- | --- |
| When do transfers begin? | Planned October 14, 2026 |
| Can I change faction? | Transfers stay within the same faction. |
| What is the initial cost? | Initially free. |
| Can an Advanced Access character move to a public-launch server? | No; Advanced Access characters are restricted to Advanced Access servers. |

Transfers retain your faction, and Advanced Access characters initially remain within the Advanced Access server pool. Choose your faction and launch server accordingly.

Public access is scheduled for **October 5 at 13:00 UTC**, after maintenance from 05:00–13:00 UTC. Check [maintenance information](/maintenance) for schedule changes before your launch session.

Read the [faction comparison](/races) before grouping up. The [server-transfer guide](/server-transfer) covers the planned October 14 opening, same-faction rule and initial Advanced Access limits.
