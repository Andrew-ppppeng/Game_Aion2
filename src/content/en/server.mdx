Choose your **region, faction, and server** together with your friends before making a character. Each Global server hosts one faction, and opposing-faction servers are paired for cross-faction play. The tables below use NC's official announcements updated on October 2, 2026; they are a dated roster and pairing snapshot, not a live population or creation-status feed. [Official server overview](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6ab85cc646be804931c31335).

<h2 id="choose-with-friends">Choose a server with your friends</h2>

1. Agree on the region first: NA West, NA East, Europe, South America, or Asia.
2. Agree on Elyos or Asmodian. Your faction determines the server list you see.
3. Check whether everyone is joining Advanced Access or public launch.
4. Choose a preferred server and a backup in that same region and faction.
5. Check the client's current creation availability before committing to the plan.

NC recommends considering a recommended server when an existing choice has a queue. A server appearing in the roster does not guarantee that character creation is open at the moment you log in. [Official queue and transfer advice](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abd2d50a279104f7d9d5ee2).

There is no supported population or latency ranking in the sources checked for this page. Pick the region that fits your group and check your own connection rather than treating an unmeasured “best server” label as a guarantee.

<h2 id="cross-server-play">What server pairing and cross-server play mean</h2>

Pairing identifies the opposing faction you meet in modes such as Spacetime Rifts and the Abyss. NC says pairings are temporary and will be adjusted periodically. The roster above therefore does not promise that your current opponent remains the same permanently. [Matchmaking explanation](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abab930eea53f5d6dbcf939).

NC separately confirms cross-server instanced content, including dungeons. That does not establish cross-region grouping or make the open world shared by every server. If you want to quest together in the open world, coordinate the same region, faction, and server. [Official cross-server statement](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abd2d50a279104f7d9d5ee2).

Use the [PvP guide](/pvp) for the distinction between modes, and the [rift guide](/spacetime-rift) for entering the opposing faction's territory.

<h2 id="transfer-rules">Transfers and public launch</h2>

| Question | Announced rule |
| --- | --- |
| When do transfers begin? | October 14, 2026 |
| Can I change faction through this transfer? | The announced transfers are within the same faction. |
| What is the initial cost? | Initially free; NC has not specified the end of the free period here. |
| Can an Advanced Access character move to a public-launch server? | No; Advanced Access characters are restricted to Advanced Access servers. |

These restrictions come from [Information on Server Transfer](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abd2d50a279104f7d9d5ee2). The announcement does not confirm cross-region transfers. Do not make a character assuming either a later faction change or unrestricted movement to a fresh launch server.

Public access is scheduled for **October 5 at 13:00 UTC**, after the planned 05:00–13:00 UTC maintenance. The maintenance window may change. Check [maintenance notices](/maintenance) and the [official access schedule](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6ab85cc646be804931c31335) before arranging your launch session.
