<h2 id="start-gathering">Start gathering on Global</h2>

On Global, gathering is available during early leveling. Start with a nearby node and read its requirement before interacting. Choose a material needed by a recipe or upgrade so your first route contributes to progression.

**KR:** the gathering unlock was raised to character level 45 as an anti-bot measure. Keep that regional rule separate from Global's early-level gathering.

Gather one accessible node, then check the material received and the proficiency gained before spending specialization points or planning a longer loop.

<h2 id="experience-specialization">Separate gathering experience, specialization and crafting</h2>

Gathering uses competing **Success** and **Failure** gauges. Track your proficiency under **Essence Extraction**. **Item Extraction** dismantles equipment and is a separate menu; gathering proficiency and crafting profession level are also separate requirements.

| System | Question to answer in your client |
| --- | --- |
| Gathering experience/proficiency | Does this node advance the same displayed bar as another resource? |
| General gathering skills | Does the tooltip affect success, speed or another gathering cost? |
| Resource specialization | Does it reveal nodes, improve yield or affect quality for your target material? |
| Crafting profession | Which recipe consumes the material, and what crafting requirements does it have? |

Read a gathering skill's effect and material coverage before investing points. Prioritize skills that improve the route you actually repeat.

<h2 id="choose-materials">Choose materials and skills for a real goal</h2>

<GuideVisual id="workflow" />

1. Open the recipe or upgrade screen you want to complete and note the missing material names and quantities.
2. Find those materials in your current region's map and check whether your character can gather them.
3. Inspect the relevant gathering skill before investing. Prefer an improvement you can use repeatedly over one for a resource you do not need.
4. Run a short trial and compare material yield, failures and time spent traveling.
5. Recheck the plan when you change crafting goals or unlock a new resource tier.

<h2 id="gathering-routes">Find nodes and build a compact route</h2>

Use [Aion2T](https://aion2t.com/map) to select your zone and filter resource markers. For elevated platforms or caves, use [Aion2Maps](https://www.aion2maps.com) to check height and access. Map markers show possible node locations; the resource may be absent when you arrive.

Build your first route around a small cluster near a familiar landmark:

- Record your faction, zone and starting point so you can repeat the same test.
- Pick one material, keep unrelated layers hidden and check terrain access.
- Note the node's displayed requirement before interacting.
- Skip missing or blocked nodes on the first pass; do not assume a fixed respawn time.
- End the test after a chosen interval and count useful materials, not just interactions.

Use the [map guide](/map) when a marker appears empty or on the wrong vertical layer. A route that requires opposite-faction travel should also account for [rift/PvP risk](/spacetime-rift).

<GuideVisual id="topic" />

**Compare a ten-minute route:** choose one needed material and three nearby accessible stops. Start at a known landmark, note successful gathers and useful yield, then count the total after ten minutes. Include travel time when deciding whether to repeat the route.

<h2 id="route-results">Check why a gathering route fails</h2>

A resource map solves **where to look**; the node tooltip and your Essence Extraction progress determine **what you can gather**. Use the [gathering map filters](/map#gathering-map-filters) to find a small cluster, then test the nodes in game.

| Result during the run | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Node is visible but gathering is unavailable | Read its exact requirement and compare it with your current proficiency |
| Attempts repeatedly fail | Check the node difficulty and your selected success-related skills before extending the route |
| The collected item does not match the quest | Compare the full name, quality and **Bound** status; do not buy a similarly named substitute |
| Most time is spent moving | Reduce the number of stops and remove repeated climbs or long detours |
| The proficiency bar stops | Check the current stage cap and promotion quest before grinding more nodes |

Keep one short record: **material needed / useful quantity / minutes / failures**. Repeat the route only if it advances the recipe or promotion you are working on. The best route for your character depends on faction, accessible nodes and current proficiency; a distant high-tier material can be a poor beginner route.

<h2 id="promotion-and-value">Resolve promotion blocks and decide whether to gather or buy</h2>

**Global, Asmodians:** at **Novice 50**, follow the promotion quest to **Alzirr in Safe Haven**. The required **Splendent Ruby (Bound)** comes from a Ruby node in **Calderon Canyon**. It is a quest item, distinct from the crafting material **Radiant Ruby Gemstone**; match the exact quest item before buying a similarly named gem.

| Situation | Practical response |
| --- | --- |
| Experience stops increasing | Check whether the current stage is capped or a promotion quest is available |
| Frequent failures | Compare your proficiency and the node requirement; inspect relevant success-related skills |
| You need one small batch immediately | Compare the actual purchase price with the time to obtain that batch |
| You will need the material repeatedly | Build a repeatable route and invest only in skills that serve it |

Compare the cost of buying a batch with the time needed to gather it. Repeated recipe demand makes a compact route more useful. Keep unfamiliar crafting materials in storage until you know their use; see the [beginner guide](/guide) for inventory priorities.
