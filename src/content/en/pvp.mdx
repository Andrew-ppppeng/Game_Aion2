AION 2 combines duels, structured matches, and contested exploration. Begin by choosing the kind of fight you want, then check that mode's rules in your client. NC officially confirms that opposing Elyos and Asmodian servers meet through modes including Spacetime Rifts and the Abyss; the detailed mode examples below are [Anders' player report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mjyZJy4XAnY), not a complete independently verified Global rulebook.

<h2 id="optional-pvp-and-danger-zones">Is PvP optional?</h2>

Anders describes ordinary open-world PvP as optional through a PvE/PvP flag, while the Abyss uses always-on PvP. That distinction matters: entering a contested zone to complete PvE objectives does not make the zone safe from player attacks.

His rift explanation also warns that enemy guard NPCs can create danger and may change your flag state. [MR4KTV's rift demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gVtMCNJgMn4) separately shows guards killing a character even while player combat is disabled. Check your flag and route before crossing into hostile territory. Avoid enemy settlements when your aim is exploration, and read the current client warning before approaching guards.

You can choose not to queue for a ranked match. For progression tasks in contested areas, weigh the reward against the risk and go with a group when you need support. The [rift guide](/spacetime-rift) explains entry, timing, and return decisions separately.

<h2 id="factions-and-server-pairings">Which opposing server will you fight?</h2>

Elyos and Asmodians live on separate faction servers. NC pairs an Elyos server with an Asmodian server for cross-faction encounters, including the Abyss. Those pairings can be changed periodically; they are not permanent rivalries. Use the [official Advanced Access Server Matchmaking notice](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abab930eea53f5d6dbcf939) to identify the published opponent for your region.

Check your region, faction, server, and the notice date together. Similar server names in another region do not identify your opponent. If you are organizing with friends, coordinate the faction and region first using the [server guide](/server). An announcement about cross-server PvE parties does not, by itself, establish shared matchmaking for every PvP mode or across regions.

<h2 id="abyss-points-and-objectives">Abyss Points and practical objectives</h2>

Anders describes Abyss Points as useful for Stigma progression and discusses medals alongside them for PvP equipment. His examples include Abyss command missions, seasonal missions, contested field bosses, Battlefield participation, and Artifact Siege. Treat these as routes to inspect in the current mission and reward windows, rather than a promise that every reward limit matches his regional experience.

For a first visit, choose one manageable objective near a safe return route. Read its completion condition and reward before accepting more tasks. If it requires player defeats, do not mistake it for a peaceful exploration mission. Compare the displayed cost of your next Stigma or equipment purchase before spending resources. The [builds guide](/builds) helps connect those choices to your actual setup.

<h3>Choose a fight that teaches the right thing</h3>

| Today's goal | Useful starting example | Check before committing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learn to react to another player | A friendly duel, as described by Anders | Agree that this is practice and test movement and recovery rather than treating the result as class balance |
| Play an objective with friends | Inspect the currently available Battlefield or other team mode | Read group-entry rules, win conditions, availability, and actual equipment treatment |
| Finish a quest across a rift | A focused route to one displayed objective | Check whether it requires player combat and avoid assuming a PvE flag protects you from guards |

A duel is useful for learning when to reach a target or disengage. It does not reproduce an objective match with allies, and a clean solo damage sequence does not establish that you can keep a team alive.

<h2 id="first-pvp-session">A plan for your first PvP session</h2>

1. Practice a duel or another available low-commitment encounter to learn target access, defense, and recovery controls.
2. Select one mode, read its current rules, and check whether you can enter with your friends.
3. Prepare reachable defensive and support buttons; do not hide every reaction inside a PvE damage sequence.
4. In a contested zone, stay with the group and identify the route back before chasing an opponent.
5. After the attempt, identify what actually caused the loss: positioning, equipment, a missed reaction, or an unclear team objective.

Use the [class comparison](/classes) for role choices and the [author tier comparison](/tier-list) for perspective. A mixed PvE ranking alone does not establish a duel or siege winner.

<h3>Two small exercises before chasing kills</h3>

For reaction practice, ask a friend to move while you try to keep target access. Then deliberately stop attacking to reach your defensive or recovery control. If that response is buried inside your PvE sequence, fix access before judging the class or equipment.

For a team attempt, read one current completion or win condition together. Decide who calls the objective and when the group regroups. Frontline players should avoid extending beyond reachable support; support players should check whether they can reach allies safely; damage dealers should follow the agreed target instead of leaving the objective to chase a distant opponent. These are practice roles, not proof of a fixed Global team composition.

**Is Battlefield always equalized?** Anders reports equalized gear and skills in his overview. Verify the currently offered mode; this page has not independently tested every Global queue.

**Can I safely explore the Abyss with PvP disabled?** Anders describes the Abyss as always-on PvP. Read the current entry warning and plan contested exploration accordingly; the ordinary-world flag is not a universal safety switch.

<h2 id="timers-and-membership-limits">Check timers and membership limits locally</h2>

Anders explicitly says the Global membership benefit for Abyss time was not confirmed in his guide. Do not budget your play around the Korean free-versus-paid hours he discusses. Likewise, check current regional event times and weekly reward counters instead of using a creator's reset schedule as your own. For commercial rules, use the separate [monetization guide](/monetization); for event availability, use the current client and official announcements.
