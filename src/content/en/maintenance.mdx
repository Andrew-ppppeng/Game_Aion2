<h2 id="check-current-status">Check the status relevant to your login</h2>

1. Open the official announcements and read the newest notice relevant to your region.
2. Check whether it describes a planned window, an active outage, or a recovery.
3. Compare the notice's date and time zone with your current time.
4. Check for an updated end time or a later recovery message.
5. If no relevant outage is announced, compare your error with the account and client checks below.

An estimated end time is a plan. A later recovery notice is stronger evidence that the event finished. Avoid treating an older “maintenance starts” message as the latest status when a recovery has already been published.

Use the [official Discord](https://discord.gg/aion2official) as an additional announcement channel. Read a regional label before applying a Korea or Taiwan maintenance post to Global; regional schedules can differ.

<h2 id="login-or-account-error">Maintenance or an account/client problem?</h2>

| What you see | Useful next action |
| --- | --- |
| A current official service interruption | Follow that notice's affected region and latest update. |
| Advanced Access entitlement rejected | Restart the client to load NC's published entitlement fix; check the account that owns access. |
| Purchase problem after a client update | Compare it with NC's purchase-fix notice and contact support if it remains unresolved. |
| A missing character after account linking changes | Follow the official linking guide instead of making more account connections. |
| Download complete but no launch access | Check Advanced Access eligibility and the public-opening schedule. |

On October 1 NC announced fixes for some Advanced Access recognition and in-game purchase problems. **That update required no downtime:** restarting the client applies it. The same notice said account-linking problems were being investigated. [Official account and purchase update](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abe2cf8a7ca1a15cf46bcbe).

NC's October 2 [linking guide](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abf40d99ed1202b9b8fb4fb) warns that separately linking another NC account can remove an existing connection and affect character access. A missing character is therefore not, by itself, evidence of maintenance. See [Steam](/steam) and [Twitch Drops](/twitch-drops) for the correct account identity.

<h2 id="report-unresolved-errors">If you still cannot connect</h2>

Record the exact error, region, server, Steam or PURPLE login method, and the time with its time zone. Restart once when following a published client-fix instruction, then check whether the error changes. Use the [download guide](/download) for launcher and installation checks.

If the official notices do not explain the failure, send that information to [AION 2 support](https://help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global). No source checked for this page provides a guaranteed support response time, a universal outage-recovery deadline, or proof that every private account issue has been fixed.
