<h2 id="check-current-status">Check the status relevant to your login</h2>

1. Open the official announcements and read the newest notice relevant to your region.
2. Check whether it describes a planned window, an active outage, or a recovery.
3. Compare the notice's date and time zone with your current time.
4. Check for an updated end time or a later recovery message.
5. If no relevant outage is announced, compare your error with the account and client checks below.

Use the newest update for the affected service. Return after a recovery notice; if maintenance is extended, use the revised window.

You can also follow the [official Discord](https://discord.gg/aion2official). Global, Korea and Taiwan have separate maintenance schedules.

<h2 id="login-or-account-error">Maintenance or an account/client problem?</h2>

| What you see | Useful next action |
| --- | --- |
| A current official service interruption | Follow that notice's affected region and latest update. |
| Advanced Access entitlement rejected | Restart and update the client; check the account that owns access. |
| Purchase problem after a client update | Keep the receipt and exact error; contact support if it persists. |
| Character missing after account-link changes | Keep existing connections and check the character’s playing account. |
| Download complete but no launch access | Check Advanced Access eligibility and the public-opening schedule. |

Restarting the client applies the fix for some Advanced Access and in-game purchase errors. **No maintenance wait is required for this update.**

Linking another NC account can remove an existing connection and affect character access. If a character disappears after account changes, keep the current connections and check its playing account. See [Steam](/steam) and [Twitch Drops](/twitch-drops).

<h2 id="report-unresolved-errors">If you still cannot connect</h2>

Record the exact error, region, server, Steam or PURPLE login method, and the time with its time zone. Restart once when following a published client-fix instruction, then check whether the error changes. Use the [download guide](/download) for launcher and installation checks.

If you still cannot connect, send these details to [AION 2 support](https://help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global).
