The current AION 2 Global Twitch promotion has **two separate campaigns**: Advanced Access on September 30–October 4 and Global launch on October 5–16. Use the matching reward table, watch a participating channel, and claim the completed Drop in Twitch inventory. [Official campaign announcement](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6ab3018d49d41d00dc124ba6).

The announcement gives date ranges without a precise start or cutoff hour for every region. Check the active campaign in [Twitch Drops inventory](https://link.twitch.tv/MyDropsInventory) for its current countdown. Do not assume that the campaign ends at your local midnight. [Official dates](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6ab3018d49d41d00dc124ba6).

NC's [Drops FAQ](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/twitchdrops/index) limits these rewards to **Global players**. Korea and Taiwan accounts are not eligible for this campaign. A stream simply showing AION 2 is not enough: it must participate in the active Drops campaign.

<h2 id="link-and-claim">Link the right account and claim</h2>

1. Open the [official Twitch Drops page](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/twitchdrops/index).
2. Check the login identity you use for AION 2. **Steam players must link Steam; PURPLE players must link their NC account.**
3. Inspect the current connection through My Page → Manage Login before changing anything.
4. Watch an eligible AION 2 channel while signed into Twitch.
5. Check inventory progress and select Claim when a reward becomes available.

NC confirms that progress can carry between participating channels in the same campaign. Switching channels does not require starting that campaign's viewing progress from zero. [Official FAQ](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/twitchdrops/index).

On October 2, NC clarified that linking a different login identity can send rewards to the wrong account. **If rewards are missing, do not separately link another account on the NC website as a quick fix.** NC says this can remove the existing connection and leave your existing character inaccessible. Follow its [account linking guide](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abf40d99ed1202b9b8fb4fb).

<h2 id="missing-drops">Troubleshoot missing progress or rewards</h2>

| Symptom | First check |
| --- | --- |
| Viewing progress does not increase | Confirm the channel participates in the active campaign and check Twitch inventory. |
| Milestone completed but not claimed | Open inventory and claim it while the campaign is available. |
| Claimed reward missing from the game | Compare the linked Steam or NC identity with the one containing your character. |
| Existing character inaccessible after account changes | Stop making additional link changes and follow NC's linking guide or support instructions. |
| A KR/TW character receives nothing | This Global campaign excludes those regional servers. |

The [official FAQ](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/twitchdrops/index) directs eligible players with missing claimed rewards to [support](https://help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global). Include the campaign, reward, claim time, game region, and login method. NC has not provided a universal delivery-time guarantee or a complete per-item character/server restriction table in the sources checked here.

In-game mail and [coupon rewards](/code) use separate claim routes. A successful claim in one system does not confirm delivery in another.
