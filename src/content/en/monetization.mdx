AION 2 Global is **free-to-play at public launch**, with optional membership, premium currency, a paid Battle Pass track, and Founder's Packs. Paying for Advanced Access and paying for ongoing trading access are separate choices. This guide distinguishes NC's published business model from current US Steam pack prices, checked October 3, 2026. [Official model](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6a4d7d47a729ca5877f5e1ef) · [Steam packs](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4972180/).

<h2 id="free-and-paid">What is free, and what requires payment?</h2>

NC's July business-model update describes these categories. The membership price below is its **published US-dollar reference**, not a verified checkout price for every country. [Official business model](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6a4d7d47a729ca5877f5e1ef).

| System | Published Global model |
| --- | --- |
| Base game | Free-to-play |
| Membership | $15/month reference; unlocks Market and Kina–Quna Exchange access plus gameplay benefits |
| Standard Battle Pass track | Available to all players |
| Premium Battle Pass track | Bought with Quna; additional rewards |
| Shop cosmetics | Cosmetic items have no combat stats |
| Shop consumables | Some assist play; the model also describes gameplay sources |

For a free-first start, wait for public access, play the basic game, and evaluate its systems before purchasing convenience or progression benefits. For trading, check the membership offer specifically: “free-to-play” does not establish unrestricted Market access. Current local taxes, currencies, and offers must be checked at checkout.

<h2 id="currency-and-trading">Kina, Quna, Market, and Exchange</h2>

| Term | Role in NC's published model |
| --- | --- |
| Kina | Gameplay-earned currency used for Market transactions |
| Quna | Premium currency for shop items and the premium pass; also participates in the Exchange |
| Market | Player item trading using Kina; active membership required |
| Exchange | Player-to-player conversion between Kina and Quna; active membership required |

NC describes a player-driven exchange: one player supplies Kina while another supplies Quna, rather than NC creating a standing currency offer. It also says Quna acquired through the Exchange can be used where Quna purchases are offered. [Official currency and economy explanation](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6a4d7d47a729ca5877f5e1ef).

Before planning a purchase, distinguish the cash-to-Quna payment from the Exchange transaction and from the later Market purchase. Check your membership, the available exchange offer, and the item price separately. This page does not provide a fixed exchange rate or a guaranteed amount of Kina per dollar.

<h2 id="passes-and-progression">Battle Pass, cosmetics, and progression</h2>

NC describes the Battle Pass as **per character**, with a free track and a Quna premium track. Its rewards include cosmetics, upgrade materials, and in-game currencies. A pass bought for one character should therefore not be budgeted as an automatically account-wide purchase. The model also distinguishes cosmetics without combat stats from other paid benefits. [Official Battle Pass and shop description](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6a4d7d47a729ca5877f5e1ef).

To judge the effect on your play, separate these questions: whether an appearance changes stats, whether payment supplies materials or convenience, and whether paid currency can reach player trading. A cosmetic-only item can coexist with other progression-related purchases. The published facts do not establish equal progression speed for every spending level or a fixed cost to finish a build.

Use [builds](/builds) to understand your character's requirements before deciding that an upgrade material is necessary. [Twitch Drops](/twitch-drops) and the [Global coupon](/code) offer separate announced rewards; they are not evidence that every shop consumable is available without limits.

<h2 id="purchase-checklist">Before paying, and if a purchase fails</h2>

1. Confirm the storefront region, currency, and final amount.
2. Check the Steam or PURPLE account containing the character you intend to use.
3. Read the offer's duration, included membership, and character restrictions.
4. For a pass, confirm the intended character and current reward track.
5. For an Exchange or Market plan, check membership and the live player offer.
6. Keep the receipt and exact error if the purchase or delivery fails.

NC announced fixes for some in-game purchase and Advanced Access recognition issues on October 1. Its fix is applied when the client restarts and does not require waiting for maintenance. [Official purchase update](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abe2cf8a7ca1a15cf46bcbe). If the issue persists, contact [support](https://help.plaync.com/faq/aion2global) with the platform, receipt, and error; this guide does not promise a refund or delivery deadline.

KR/TW economy guides describe other operating versions. Use Global offer descriptions for this launch rather than importing regional prices, older membership allowances, or creator estimates of spending efficiency.
