Spacetime Rifts connect you to the opposing faction's territory for exploration and combat. Before entering, check three separate things: when the entrance appears, how long it accepts players, and how long your character may remain inside. NC confirms that rifts use [paired Elyos and Asmodian servers](https://aion2.plaync.com/en-us/board/notice/view?articleId=6abab930eea53f5d6dbcf939); a fixed Global timetable has not been verified for this page.

<h2 id="what-rifts-connect">What a rift connects</h2>

Your faction lives on its own server. The opposing players you meet through cross-faction modes come from the server currently matched with yours, rather than from a second faction living on your home server. NC can update those pairings, so check the notice and your region before organizing a trip.

Rifts also serve exploration. [MR4KTV's pre-Global demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gVtMCNJgMn4) shows enemy-territory quests, Sealed Dungeons, and Strongholds. Use that demonstration to understand the purpose of a visit, then inspect your current map and quest rewards. Do not assume that every regional reward or later activity exists in your Global client.

<h2 id="check-the-three-timers">Check the three timers</h2>

| Timer | What you need to know | How to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Next opening | When an entrance becomes available in your region | Set your reminder using a labeled timezone |
| Entrance window | How long you can cross after it opens | Arrive before the window closes, not at the end of your planned play session |
| Stay duration | How much time remains after entry | Choose objectives that fit and plan the return |

Older player reports disagree. [RedCloud's Korean-server guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8rK7Wo37_X8) reports openings every four hours and a five-minute entrance window. [Madsin's later systems overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tn7WKMXt5Wo) reports every three hours, a ten-minute entrance window, and a one-hour stay. Those are reported versions, not a combined Global schedule.

Use the live event indicator and entry information in your client as the deciding reference. If a timer and the game disagree, first check region, timezone, and whether the countdown describes entry or remaining stay. A browser's local clock alone does not establish the server event timezone.

<h2 id="external-timers">Use an external timer with the right region</h2>

[TalentBuilds' rift timer](https://talentbuilds.com/aion2/spacetimerift) provides region selectors, but the text-only view checked for this page still showed loading schedule entries and older launch wording. It is a third-party aid, not an official Global timetable.

The [AION 2 Tracker author's Global update](https://www.reddit.com/r/Aion2/comments/1wv1wrx/aion_2_tracker_global_release_update/) says its rift timing was adjusted after Early Access experience. You can inspect the linked [tracker](https://aion2tracker.net/) as another aid. That is the author's report; this wiki has not validated every countdown against same-day client observations.

Select your region, read the displayed timezone, and compare one opening with the game before relying on reminders. If your exact region is absent or the schedule does not load, use the client indicator rather than selecting the closest-looking region.

<h3>A useful record from your first opening</h3>

| Record this | Where to verify it | What it helps you decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Region, server, date, and timezone | Your server context and the timer's visible timezone | Whether the external countdown refers to your event |
| Entrance opening and closing information | The active marker and current entry information | When you need to reach the entrance |
| Remaining stay after crossing | The event information available inside | Which nearby objectives fit the visit |
| Available return or revival destination | The current exit or revival information | Whether choosing it ends the trip or leaves you in danger |

Write what you actually observe; leave an unknown field unconfirmed. One observed opening does not establish a recurring interval. Compare a subsequent opening before building a repeating reminder around it.

<h2 id="find-and-enter-the-rift">Find and enter the rift</h2>

1. Pick one objective: a quest, an exploration area, or a combat outing.
2. Check the active rift marker on your current faction map and plan a route to it.
3. Confirm the entrance's actual position and height. A map icon alone may not show a flyable approach or the correct level of terrain.
4. Arrive early, inspect the entry information, and check your combat flag before crossing.
5. On the other side, note your remaining time and a safe route toward the objective.

MR4KTV demonstrates visible map markers and traveling to the entrance. RedCloud shows an entrance location on his Korean Asmodian map, but that is not a verified coordinate for every region or faction. Use the [map guide](/map) to inspect layers and paths; this page does not invent a universal entrance coordinate.

<h2 id="guards-death-and-return">Guards, death, and your return plan</h2>

A PvE flag is not protection from hostile NPCs. MR4KTV's [demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gVtMCNJgMn4) shows enemy guards killing the character and a normal return ending the visit. Keep clear of enemy bases when you are there for PvE objectives, and check the available revival option before accepting it.

Madsin describes Kisk use as an alternative return arrangement in his version. Read the current item's placement and revival rules before relying on it; having an item in your inventory does not establish a working return point. Bring an appropriate revival resource only after checking its current description and availability. Do not spend it reflexively to stand up in front of the same guards.

<h3>Three common problems and the next useful check</h3>

**The tracker says open, but the client does not.** Compare region and timezone first, then check whether one counter describes entry and the other describes your stay. Use the live client indicator for the attempt and record the mismatch rather than selecting another region to make the countdown look right.

**The map marker is visible, but you cannot reach the entrance.** Check the current terrain level and approach. A marker on the map is not a universal coordinate or a guarantee that you can enter from the position directly underneath it. If the entrance window has closed, prepare the route for a later confirmed opening.

**You died near guards and see a revival choice.** Read its destination before accepting. MR4KTV's demonstration shows an ordinary return ending the visit; Madsin's Kisk discussion belongs to his reported version. Do not spend a revival resource solely because you hope it preserves the visit.

**Does the entrance closing mean my remaining stay is over?** These are separate timings. Read the remaining-stay information inside rather than using the entrance countdown to estimate how much time your character has left.

**Is a PvE flag enough for a peaceful visit?** It does not protect from hostile NPCs in the demonstrated regional footage. Choose an exploration route away from enemy settlements and verify current combat warnings.

<h2 id="plan-a-useful-visit">Plan a useful first visit</h2>

Start with a small route of nearby objectives, leaving time for movement and unexpected danger. Check quest rewards, Stronghold completion, and Sealed Dungeon progress in your own interface. Record unfinished locations for the next confirmed opening rather than rushing across the whole map.

If your aim is player combat, prepare a [PvP setup](/pvp) and coordinate with your group. If your aim is progression, keep the route focused, monitor the flag and timer, and avoid unnecessary fights. Recheck the [server pairing](/server) and schedule after regional updates instead of treating yesterday's opponent or an older Korean timer as permanent.
