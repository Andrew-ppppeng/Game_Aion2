<h2 id="global-leveling">What is the AION 2 max level?</h2>

**Global's launch max level is 45. KR/TW Chapter 1 raises the character cap to 50.**

| Service | Character level cap | Route to follow |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Global launch | 45 | Your faction's 1–45 campaign |
| KR/TW, Chapter 1 | 50 | Continue into Eltnen or Morheim after the earlier campaign |

Character level, skill level, and gathering proficiency use separate progression. The level-50 KR/TW route is not the Global launch route. For collection and extraction progression, use the [gathering guide](/gathering); for skill upgrades, use [builds](/builds).

Follow the main story and complete nearby objectives when they meet the next progression requirement. Finish remaining story unlocks after reaching the cap.

Keep three goals separate: **character level** advances with experience, **item level/gear score** can gate equipment content, and **Combat Power** reflects wider character strength. An equipment upgrade may help you beat a quest boss without increasing your character's experience bar.

**Start the route:** select the main campaign objective in your journal, track its destination, and use the navigation controls. Group nearby objectives to reduce travel, but stop to handle dangerous terrain or a progression gate.

<h2 id="elyos-route">Elyos: main story first, nearby requirements second</h2>

Follow the Elyos campaign through Verteron, including destinations around Cantas Valley and Dawn Legion Base. Keep the next story objective active so optional quests stay aligned with your route.

1. Finish the introductory story and follow the next campaign objective rather than clearing every optional marker.
2. In Verteron, take nearby regional quests when they satisfy a displayed progression requirement or offer a useful reward.
3. When Ascension blocks the story, inspect its objective/gauge, complete eligible nearby content and return to the campaign.
4. Equip useful weapon and accessory rewards, then review newly available skill options.
5. After reaching 45, finish remaining story objectives before treating the character as ready for every endgame activity.

**TW Elyos route checkpoints:** use these level ranges to see when nearby side quests, Ascension, and equipment reviews fit into a story-led run. On Global, follow the requirement shown in your quest journal.

<h2 id="asmodian-route">Asmodians: follow the Altgard campaign</h2>

Follow the Asmodian campaign through Altgard, with destinations around Safe Haven, Moslan Forest, and the Nornir Assembly. Track your own faction’s objective to keep travel and nearby tasks together.

- During the opening, complete the story's introductions and access requirements in order.
- In Altgard, follow your active campaign destination and combine nearby eligible side objectives when a gate appears.
- Check the progression/Ascension panel at a story block and use the content your current client accepts for it.
- Inspect sealed dungeons or regional objectives that provide a useful unlock or reward; combine them with nearby campaign objectives.
- Approaching 45, finish the remaining campaign and inspect the entry panel for your next activity.

Use the [map guide](/map) to organize travel points, nearby objectives, and unfinished exploration along your campaign route.

<h2 id="dungeon-checkpoints">Dungeon progression checkpoints</h2>

Use these Global Exploration milestones to review your skills, equipment, and next unlock.

| Character level | Dungeon milestone | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 | First Exploration expedition | Check its actual unlock quest and entry requirements |
| 28 | Urugugu Canyon | Learn the encounter while progressing the story |
| 35 | Fire Temple | Review skills and mechanics before attempting it |
| 45 | Further expeditions and maximum-level progression | Complete remaining story and check the available mode |

Dungeon entry combines character level, quest completion, and equipment requirements. At each milestone, open the dungeon panel and satisfy the listed conditions. Repeat an activity when it supplies the experience, unlock, or reward you need.

**If entry is denied at a listed level:** check the unlock quest and item requirement separately. Upgrading equipment solves an equipment gate; completing a missing quest solves the unlock.

<h2 id="leveling-blockers">Troubleshoot a stalled route</h2>

| Symptom | Check first | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No next story quest | Journal, preceding turn-in and required level | Complete the missing objective or nearby eligible experience content |
| Progression gauge is short | Current Ascension instructions | Use accepted regional content instead of unrelated monster grinding |
| A boss takes too long | Weapon, active skills and missed mechanics | Equip relevant rewards and adjust your [build](/builds) |
| Dungeon entry is denied | Current quest, level and item requirement | Satisfy the listed gate |
| Travel consumes most of the session | Activated travel points and terrain | Use the [map](/map), group nearby objectives and review flight bindings |

Keep damage skills updated, equip useful quest rewards, and collect nearby feathers while already following the route. These upgrades reduce combat downtime without adding a long detour.

<h2 id="after-leveling">What to do after reaching 45</h2>

Finish the remaining story, review available progression tasks, and identify the next entry requirement. Complete accessible unfinished exploration for its rewards and choose the improvement that helps your next goal.

Choose one next goal: a stronger skill setup, an unfinished collection area, a dungeon unlock or a needed equipment improvement. Use [beginner priorities](/guide) and [builds](/builds) to organize that transition. Build toward the entry requirement and mechanics of your next dungeon.

<h3>How long does it take to reach max level?</h3>

There is no fixed completion time. Travel, side objectives, progression gates and time spent learning your class change the length of the run. Plan a session around the next campaign unlock rather than a promised number of hours.

<h3>Why does a level-45 character still have progression left?</h3>

The character-level cap does not complete unfinished quests or meet every dungeon's equipment requirement. Use this order:

1. Finish pending campaign objectives and turn-ins.
2. Open the entry panel for the activity you want to play and identify the missing quest or equipment condition.
3. Review active skills and equipped options for that activity.
4. Choose accessible content that supplies the specific unlock or equipment improvement you need.
